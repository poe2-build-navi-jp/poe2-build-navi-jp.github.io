# Tier 16 Waystone guide review

Prepared 2026-10-25. Draft only; publication requires fresh approval for this article.

## Scope and existing content

`waystones-tablets` covers identification, Map Device and Tablet troubleshooting. `atlas-progression` covers Fortress/quest progression. The new `tier-16-waystones` guide owns obtaining T16, corruption preparation, T15 supply, trade comparison and item-tier/area-level distinctions. Only the existing Waystone guide receives a related link; hub, search and sitemaps are generated.

## Evidence decisions

- GGG 0.1.1 explicitly mentions T15 corruption into T16 and fixes an unintended Arbiter of Ash T16 drop. It is identified as historical evidence, not a current patch.
- Current PoE2DB Corrupted page corroborates T15 corruption. Its brief outcome list is not treated as exhaustive or an equal-probability distribution.
- Current Reforging Bench recipes stop at three T14 to one T15. No three-T15 recipe is asserted.
- Current Waystone data supplies base area levels (T14 78, T15 79, T16 80) and supply mechanics. General higher-tier drop language is not extrapolated into guaranteed T16 drops.
- GGG 0.5.0 supports identification, revised modifier categories/rewards, and discontinued new acquisition of Omen of Corruption. Existing Standard items are distinguished.
- Temple T3/T4 corruption targets equipment/jewels; this is not a failed-Waystone retry method. Chisel is marked Not in game.
- Sources are linked per claim; PoE2DB is labelled nonofficial. No copied guide wording or invented player experience.
- No exact probability, expected attempt count/cost, fixed prices, exhaustive special-drop rule, or six-mod success bonus. Trade UI and blocked Wiki content were not fetched around restrictions.
