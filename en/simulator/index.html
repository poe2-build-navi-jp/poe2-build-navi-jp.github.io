Change from starting values
Latest reviewed patch: 0.5.5f Hotfix

Official patch review: 2026-11-11. This is a review of official patch changes, not a full retest or an in-game playtest of every build.

  • 0.5.5f Hotfix(published November 10, 2026)

    Fixed an issue preventing rewards after completing a series of maps selected with a Corrupted Precursor Beacon. This does not apply if the original affected map is already open.

    A reward and progression change, not evidence for changing build ratings.

    The issue where rewards are not granted after failing the final map and retrying is reported as under investigation.

  • 0.5.5f(published November 6, 2026)

    Support for upcoming announcements and new supporter packs.

    The published notes do not list changes to skill or passive values.

    An Xbox release delay was announced at publication; this review did not establish when it was resolved.

  • 0.5.5e Hotfix 1(published October 28, 2026)

    Fixed incorrect minimap terrain display. The notice instructs players to restart the client to receive the update.

    A display fix, recorded separately from changes to levelling guidance.

Published builds use version 0.5.5 as their baseline; engine data is version 0.5.5.5. Only reviewed changes are reflected, and source review dates remain on individual guides.
