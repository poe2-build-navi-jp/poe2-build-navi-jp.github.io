LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Plant Oracle leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 8 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–10You are here
  1. Place Entangle in the enemy's path
  2. Look for a weapon with +spell levels
  3. Check Life, resistances and movement speed
Main skill
Entangle. On bosses, also use whatever single-target support spell you have.
Supports
Entangle is usable from Lv1. The source's Skill Gems section shows a Lv20 gem setup example; it does not mean all listed supports can be obtained at character Lv1. Check the early acquisition slots in the source's leveling steps.
Passives
Use the source's early stage-by-stage tree. Check the exact node names at the linked source.
Gear priorities
+physical / all spell levels, spell / physical spell power, Life, ES or Armour, resistances, movement speed.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize comparing weapons without +spell levels or spell power.
Caution
Entangle has a time lag before its damage occurs. Place it at the enemy's feet, then take distance.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv11. You can handle regular enemies with Entangle and use a support skill on strong enemies.

Entangle + manual Thunderstorm. On strong enemies, layer Thunderstorm over the plants, and prepare a single-target support tool as well.

BUILD PROFILE

Plant Oracle: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Entangle can be kept as the main skill from Lv1 through Endgame.
  • The basic clearing actions are easy to keep few, and plant-based rooting can also be used.
  • A leveling guide and an Endgame guide are published by the same author.
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) lists "easy map clear," "auto-targeting" and "cheap to start at league start" as strengths.
  • High pack-clearing ability and few actions to perform (Gamerch).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Defense is gear-dependent, and ES and chaos resistance must be sorted out later on.
  • Watch out for running out of mana in long boss fights.
  • The triggered setup from the second ascendancy onward must only be switched to once the required gems and Spirit are ready.
  • Damage is hard to land on bosses that keep moving, and burst damage is on the low side (Gamerch). Maxroll also says top-tier bosses require gear investment.
  • The screen tends to become hard to read (Maxroll weakness: Visual Clutter).
  • Whether it works in SSF is not stated in the sources. Maxroll lists being cheap to start as a strength, while listing that top-tier bosses require gear investment as a weakness.

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon (staff): among suffixes, +levels to physical spell skills is the top priority, followed by critical strike chance, critical damage bonus and cast speed. Prefixes: increased physical spell damage, "gain damage as extra damage," and increased spell damage (Gamerch).
  2. Campaign: check shops for weapons with +physical spell / +spell skill levels. Maxroll uses a Life-based setup that reduces damage taken with Armour and "Armour also applies to elemental damage."
  3. Armor: ES, movement speed on boots, Spirit on the chest, maximum mana. Gloves take critical damage. Secure resistances including chaos resistance (Gamerch).
  4. Amulet: ES, +spell skill levels, critical damage, resistances (Gamerch).
  5. Cheap uniques: the Nightscale gloves supplement critical strike chance and mana recovery and can be used from leveling onward. The Waveshaper chest can be used from leveling through Endgame (Gamerch).
  6. Endgame: ES, chaos resistance, Spirit, mana sustain. Maxroll's Endgame setup casts spells using Life (with the Atalui's Bloodletting and Lifetap support gems) and runs Mind Over Matter together with Eldritch Battery.
