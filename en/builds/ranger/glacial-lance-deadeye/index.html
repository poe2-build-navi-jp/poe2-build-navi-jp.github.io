LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Glacial Lance Deadeye leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Act 1 (Twister leveling)You are here
  1. Keep Whirling Slash at Lv1
  2. Fire Twister from inside the whirlwind
  3. Update the spear every 5–10 levels
Main skill
Twister + Whirling Slash. On bosses, Parry → Fangs of Frost to create chilled ground, then attack.
Supports
Frost Nexus generates chilled ground. Keep Whirling Slash fixed at Lv1 to save mana (source). War Banner is also used for single target.
Passives
Push damage first early on. Critical is hard to scale, so leave it for later (source). See the source for the per-stage tree.
Gear priorities
Life and resistances on most slots. Large added damage on rings, spear and gloves (any element). Update the spear every 5–10 levels, prioritizing physical and cold damage and attack speed suffixes.
Replacement candidates
Update the spear with top priority. Also review rings and gloves with no added damage.
Caution
If you leave Whirling Slash's whirlwind and fire Twister, the whirlwind collapses and you lose the added damage. Always fire from inside the whirlwind.
When to move to the next stage
Act 2 begins. Barrage and Ice-Tipped Arrows can be prepared.

Early Act 2 – First Trial (continue Twister)You are here
  1. Add Barrage and Ice-Tipped Arrows
  2. Use Freezing Mark on tough enemies
  3. Clear the first trial
Main skill
Continue Twister. Barrage consumes Frenzy Charges to add projectiles, and Ice-Tipped Arrows is used on cooldown.
Supports
Ice-Tipped Arrows' ice shards speed up pack clear, so Whirling Slash is used less often. Use Freezing Mark on tough rares and bosses, and Charged Mark also applies shocked ground.
Passives
Continue damage first, and do not lean into critical yet (source).
Gear priorities
Continue updating the spear. Maintain Life and resistances.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize reviewing a spear whose damage has stalled and armour pieces with low resistances.
Caution
Parry and Fangs of Frost can be removed at the start of Act 2 (source). Do not move to Glacial Lance until the first trial is finished.
When to move to the next stage
Clear the first trial, making Glacial Lance (Tier 7, from Lv22) and the Mirage Deadeye ascendancy available.
Source stage: Continue damage first, and do not lean into critical yet (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
After the First Trial – Campaign End (switch to Glacial Lance)You are here
  1. Switch to Glacial Lance after the first trial
  2. Prepare a Tier 8 Spirit gem and enable Mirage Archer
  3. On bosses, use Pounce → Arctic Howl in that order
Main skill
Glacial Lance. Attacks summon Mirage Deadeye, and dodge rolls summon Mirage Archer.
Supports
Mirage Archer requires a Tier 8 Spirit gem (poe2db; the source writes Tier 7+). In boss fights, place War Banner, apply Freezing Mark with Pounce, and once freeze is primed, freeze with Arctic Howl and trigger Raging Cry. Fire Barrage with Combat Frenzy's Frenzy Charges.
Passives
Ascendancy order: Mirage Deadeye → Gathering Winds → Called Shots → Avidity (source). The tree continues damage first.
Gear priorities
Sceptre: a Shrine Sceptre (resistances) or an Omen Sceptre (boss critical chance). Attack speed and damage on the Weapon Set 2 talisman also grow Pounce and Arctic Howl (lower priority). Fill attribute and resistance shortfalls with runes.
Replacement candidates
Continue updating the spear. If Spirit is short, secure it with the sceptre and amulet.
Caution
Pounce requires a talisman in Weapon Set 2's off-hand. Use both mirages on cooldown.
When to move to the next stage
Campaign finished. Glacial Lance and both mirages are operated stably.

Early MapsYou are here
  1. Socket the Idol of Eramir into the sceptre
  2. Secure 240 Spirit from the amulet and sceptre
  3. Make the helmet pure ES in preparation for Subterfuge Mask
Main skill
Glacial Lance + both mirages. Mana Remnants recover mana and speed up mirage re-summoning.
Supports
Adding Khatal's Rejuvenation to Mana Remnants makes it easier to maintain 40% cooldown recovery speed. War Banner can gain Deflect with Refraction II (requires 10 additional Spirit). Ghost Dance also recovers ES based on Evasion (per the source's introduction).
Passives
Prioritize Frenzy Charge nodes until the Idol of Eramir is socketed into the sceptre. Critical comes after a good spear is crafted (source).
Gear priorities
Right after reaching maps, the top damage priority is the Idol of Eramir socketed into the sceptre (charge generation effectively doubles). Stack Evasion, ES and Deflect on chest, gloves, boots and helmet, and make the helmet pure ES (preparation for Subterfuge Mask). Prepare Valako's Roar charm early as well. Secure a total of 240 Spirit from the amulet and sceptre.
Replacement candidates
Do not socket an expensive Idol of Eramir into a bad sceptre. Choose the sceptre that receives the idol carefully.
Caution
Using Barrage with Olroth's Conviction requires Ward on gear, with a total of 1.5–2× the cost as the guideline (source FAQ). Instilling Thaumaturgic Generator is expensive, so do not put it on an amulet you will replace soon.
When to move to the next stage
Low-Tier maps can be run stably, and the Idol of Eramir and 240 Spirit are in place.
Source stage: Prioritize Frenzy Charge nodes until the Idol of Eramir is socketed into the sceptre. Critical comes after a good spear is crafted (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
EndgameYou are here
  1. Pick up and craft an Akoyan Spear at area Lv78
  2. Aim for a Sacred Flame sceptre
  3. Check the transition conditions for Guiding Palm of the Eye (40 Spirit on the amulet)
Main skill
Glacial Lance + Barrage. The stage where you scale with critical.
Supports
Adding Olroth's Conviction to Barrage lets you use it 3 times, making it effectively always available (Ward required). Moving from a Shrine Sceptre with Purity of Ice to Guiding Palm of the Eye lets you socket Vitality II and Cannibalism II for free.
Passives
Once a good spear with a critical strike chance roll is crafted, take all the critical nodes. Take Subterfuge Mask only after a good ES helmet is crafted (source).
Gear priorities
Pick up an Akoyan Spear that drops at area Lv78, create two good mods with Transmutation and Augmentation Orbs, and make it 4-mod with an essence and a Preserved Jawbone. The Sacred Flame sceptre is a big gain. Overflow mana with Uhtred's Chalice and Mana Remnants.
Replacement candidates
Compare the spear's critical strike chance roll with top priority. The finished belt is pursued in the next stage.
Caution
Critical assumes weapon quality. Do not lean into critical nodes while you have no good spear.
When to move to the next stage
The Akoyan Spear craft progresses and the critical setup is stable.
Source stage: Once a good spear with a critical strike chance roll is crafted, take all the critical nodes. Take Subterfuge Mask only after a good ES helmet is crafted (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (Aspirational)You are here
  1. Aim for the finished form's unique belt
  2. Check the Aspirational tree's jewel prerequisites
  3. If adopting Rakiata's Flow, remove elemental penetration
Main skill
Glacial Lance. High-speed mapping, firing continuously while moving.
Supports
Combining Rite of Passage with Spirit of the Wolf lets all damage break Armour, and spreads Herald of Ice and Armour Explosion. The Fall of the Axe grants slow prevention and Onslaught.
Passives
The source's Aspirational tree. Hail assumes Barrage and Olroth's Conviction. Take Crimson Assault via Controlled Metamorphosis, a Time-Lost Emerald and From Nothing. On the assumption of using Rakiata's Flow, remove all investment into elemental penetration. Put spare attributes into Strength (Life).
Gear priorities
The belt reaches its full value with Mageblood, and Headhunter is also usable. Stack movement speed, attack speed and Evasion heavily.
Replacement candidates
Not by fixed price; update one slot at a time by the performance difference against your current gear.
Caution
The Aspirational tree assumes multiple jewels and includes Controlled Metamorphosis, which lowers resistances. Do not lean into it before gear is in place.
Completion condition
Once finished, improve the bottlenecks among spear, charge generation and defense one item at a time.
Source stage: The source's Aspirational tree. Hail assumes Barrage and Olroth's Conviction. Take Crimson Assault via Controlled Metamorphosis, a Time-Lost Emerald and From Nothing. BUILD PROFILE

Glacial Lance Deadeye: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • You can clear while freezing the whole screen, with fast clear speed (source strengths: Great Clear Speed / Full Screen Freezes).
  • Mirage Deadeye and Mirage Archer help with both clear and single-target attacks (per the source's introduction and summary).
  • Single-target damage is also good, and large bosses can be brought down with Barrage's rapid fire (source strength: Good Boss Damage).
  • It functions without uniques, and you can play safely behind defensive layers of freeze, Evasion, Deflect and ES (per the source's introduction and its summary, "safe and fast league starter").

Weaknesses & cautions

  • It is not designed to keep taking boss attacks head-on (source weakness: Not Made To Facetank Bosses).
  • Deep Endgame needs gear (source weakness: Needs Some Gear for Deep Endgame). The Idol of Eramir and Thaumaturgic Generator instillations tend to be expensive.
  • The signature Glacial Lance is a Tier 7 gem, from Lv22. You level with Twister until the first trial in Act 2.
  • Running Barrage with Olroth's Conviction requires Ward on gear, and it stalls if you do not meet the guideline of 1.5–2× the cost (source FAQ).

Gear priorities

  1. Spear: added physical and cold damage and attack speed. Update every 5–10 levels; the finished form is a crafted Akoyan Spear.
  2. Sceptre: right after reaching maps, the base for socketing the Idol of Eramir. Together with the amulet, secure 240 Spirit.
  3. Armour: Evasion, ES and Deflect. The helmet is pure ES (for Subterfuge Mask). Rings and gloves take added damage.

FAQ

Glacial Lance Deadeye build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who want to map at high speed alongside mirages while freezing the whole screen.

What weakness should I know before starting?

It is not designed to keep taking boss attacks head-on (source weakness: Not Made To Facetank Bosses).
