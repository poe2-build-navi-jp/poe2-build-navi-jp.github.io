LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Twister Deadeye leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–Act 1 (start Twister leveling)You are here
  1. Create a Huntress mule and pass Whirling Slash, a spear and a buckler through the stash (without a mule, level with Lightning Arrow to around Lv10)
  2. Keep Whirling Slash at Lv1 and progress with the procedure vortex ×3 → Twister
  3. Parry with the buckler → create cold ground with Fangs of Frost to strengthen Twister
Main skill
Stack Whirling Slash's wind vortex up to 3 layers and consume it with Twister to strengthen it. Early on, use the buckler to Parry → Fangs of Frost to create cold ground.
Supports
Whirling Slash is kept at Lv1: Rapid Attacks I → Rage I. Twister: Retreat → Prolonged Duration I. Ice-Tipped Arrows: Elemental Armament I. War Banner: Prolonged Duration I. Frost Bomb is also used. Setup from the source planner (Campaign).
Passives
Follow the source's "Campaign" tree. Take enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements, and put the rest into Strength to grow Life. Weapon set point allocation starts in Act 2.
Gear priorities
Weapon Set 1 is a spear prioritizing attack speed (Winged Spear or Soaring Spear, with an attack speed suffix; Skysliver if you have it). Leave the off-hand empty to receive Dance with Death's benefit. Weapon Set 2 is a damage-focused spear. Armour pieces are Armour-focused in Acts 1–3 to suppress damage taken while parrying.
Replacement candidates
When a spear with an attack speed suffix appears, update Weapon Set 1 with it. Once you take the second Uncut Spirit Gem (Lv6), replace the buckler with an Omen Sceptre.
Caution
To level with Twister from Lv1 in a fresh league, you must first create a Huntress mule character and pass a Lv1 Whirling Slash, a spear and a buckler through the stash. Without a mule, level with Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod and switch around Lv10, once you have 3 Lv3 gems and a decent spear. Keeping Whirling Slash at Lv1 is a strong instruction in the source; leveling it only increases mana cost. WASD movement is the source's recommendation; with mouse movement you must keep the cursor near your character, which is awkward to operate.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Act 2 and cut Frost Nexus with the first Lv2 Uncut Support Gem.
Source stage: Follow the source's "Campaign" tree. Take enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements, and put the rest into Strength to grow Life. Weapon set point allocation starts in Act 2.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Act 2 (switch to Frost Nexus)You are here
  1. Cut Frost Nexus with the first Lv2 support gem and remove Fangs of Frost
  2. Switch to an Omen Sceptre with the second Spirit Gem (Lv6)
  3. Take the Gathering Winds → Avidity ascendancy in the first trial
Main skill
The basic Twister + Whirling Slash rotation. Cull the Weak generates Frenzy Charges while moving, and Freezing Mark freezes tough enemies.
Supports
Twister: Retreat → Frost Nexus → Elemental Armament II. Ice-Tipped Arrows: Elemental Armament I → Magnified Area I. Barrage: Rapid Casting → Perpetual Charge. Cull the Weak: Thrill of the Kill II → Charge Profusion. Freezing Mark: Prolonged Duration I. Herald of Ice: Elemental Armament II → Magnified Area II. Setup from the source planner (Maps).
Passives
Start weapon set point allocation in Act 2. Weapon Set 1 is Whirling Slash and Barrage; Weapon Set 2 is everything else. Ice-Tipped Arrows must be placed on Weapon Set 2 (otherwise it does not function). The first ascendancy is Gathering Winds → Avidity to grow Frenzy Charge generation.
Gear priorities
Once you take the second Uncut Spirit Gem (Lv6), switch to an Omen Sceptre. Armour pieces stay Armour-focused, and from Act 3 you will also want Evasion. Jewellery and gloves prioritize added damage to attacks (flat damage).
Replacement candidates
Equip the Omen Sceptre in Weapon Set 2's off-hand. When a high-attack-speed spear appears, update Weapon Set 1 with it.
Caution
Once you cut Frost Nexus, it creates the cold ground, so Fangs of Frost can be removed. Use Ice-Tipped Arrows on cooldown, converting damage to cold to freeze. Cull the Weak is used for Frenzy Charge generation while moving, and against rare or magic packs you fire a Frenzy-empowered Barrage. On bosses, Freezing Mark creates ice ground together with Frost Nexus.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Act 3 and cut Herald of Ice.
Source stage: Start weapon set point allocation in Act 2. Weapon Set 1 is Whirling Slash and Barrage; Weapon Set 2 is everything else. Ice-Tipped Arrows must be placed on Weapon Set 2 (otherwise it does not function). The first ascendancy is Gathering Winds → Avidity to grow Frenzy Charge generation.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Act 3–Campaign End (Herald of Ice)You are here
  1. Cut Herald of Ice to raise clear
  2. Once a chest with 1200+ Evasion appears, start taking Deflection nodes
  3. Take Thrilling Chase with the third ascendancy
Main skill
The basic rotation (Ice-Tipped Arrows → Whirling Slash ×3 → Barrage → Twister) gains Herald of Ice's explosions.
Supports
Twister: continue Retreat → Frost Nexus → Elemental Armament II. Extend Herald of Ice to Elemental Armament II → Magnified Area II → Elemental Focus. Link Charge Profusion to Combat Frenzy. See the source planner for the exact slots.
Passives
The third ascendancy is Thrilling Chase. Once you obtain an Evasion chest (1200+ Evasion Rating), start taking Deflection nodes such as The Wild Cat / Trained Deflection / Enhanced Reflexes. Projectile speed, skill effect duration and area of effect become Twister's damage multipliers.
Gear priorities
From Act 3, Evasion becomes desirable. On armour pieces, prioritize Life and resistances while combining Armour and Evasion. Put a high-physical-damage spear on Weapon Set 2.
Replacement candidates
Once a chest with an Evasion Rating of 1200+ appears, switch defenses together with the Deflection nodes. Jewellery prioritizes added damage to attacks.
Caution
Cutting Herald of Ice makes clear much easier, so do not neglect carving out the support slots. If landing criticals on the marked target with Whirling Slash is difficult, check Pinpoint Critical, Inexorable Critical II and a Rune of Consistency on the helmet.
When to move to the next stage
Campaign finished and maps reached.

Early MapsYou are here
  1. Obtain boots that create elemental ground and do without Frost Nexus
  2. Buy The Taming with top priority
  3. Take Called Shots with the fourth ascendancy and switch to Sniper's Mark
Main skill
The Twister + Barrage Salvo combination. Called Shots automatically applies Sniper's Mark to generate Frenzy Charges.
Supports
Twister: Retreat → Frost Nexus → Elemental Armament II → Acceleration → Deliberation. Barrage: Perpetual Charge → Rapid Casting II → Heightened Charges. Release Salvo only after storing 4+ seals. Called Shots' Sniper's Mark: Charged Mark → Eternal Mark → Ingenuity → Charge Profusion. See the source planner for the exact slots.
Passives
The fourth ascendancy is Called Shots. Replace Freezing Mark with Sniper's Mark, and grow Frenzy generation with Eternal Mark (only when your critical strike chance is sufficient to consume them stably). Deflection nodes are brought into full use with a chest of 1500+ Evasion.
Gear priorities
Wake of Destruction creates elemental ground, so you no longer need Frost Nexus. The top purchase priority is The Taming, which lets you always strengthen Twister even without elemental ground. Serpent's Egg is good for strengthening Frenzy Charge generation. Jewellery covers mana with Sapphires granting 2% mana recovery on kill.
Replacement candidates
Obtain Wake of Destruction early, then buy The Taming with top priority. Berek's Grip / Berek's Pass / Berek's Respite are stopgaps until The Taming.
Caution
Do not fire Twister too often. The source instructs you to wait for the combination layered with Salvo and Barrage before releasing it. Once Called Shots applies a mark, trigger it twice with Whirling Slash to generate up to 4 Frenzy Charges. On bosses, store 4–6 Frenzy Charges, then release the Salvo + Barrage + Twister combination.
When to move to the next stage
Obtain The Taming, with a prospect of obtaining Olroth's Conviction.
Source stage: The fourth ascendancy is Called Shots. Replace Freezing Mark with Sniper's Mark, and grow Frenzy generation with Eternal Mark (only when your critical strike chance is sufficient to consume them stably). Deflection nodes are brought into full use with a chest of 1500+ Evasion.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (Crit)You are here
  1. Obtain with top priority the talisman that lets Barrage be used 3 times on cooldown, and remove Salvo from Twister
  2. Balance Trinity's elements and reach the Lv23 breakpoint
  3. Generate Power Charges with Spear Stab + Ailith's Chimes
Main skill
The Twister + Barrage critical setup. Generate Power Charges with Spear Stab + Ailith's Chimes and extend them with Charge Regulation.
Supports
Barrage: Perpetual Charge → Rapid Casting II → Heightened Charges → Cooldown Recovery II → Efficiency II. Twister: Retreat → Acceleration → Rakiata's Flow → Deliberation → Salvo. Once Olroth's Conviction is in, remove Salvo from Twister. Whirling Slash: Rapid Attacks III → Rage III → Pinpoint Critical → Inexorable Critical II → Blazing Critical. Spear Stab: Culmination II → Ailith's Chimes → Charge Profusion → Rapid Attacks III → Rage III. See the source planner for the exact slots.
Passives
Balance critical nodes with Evasion / Deflection nodes. Deflection nodes are brought into full use with a chest of 1500+ Evasion. Balance Trinity with Heart of the Well's "gain damage as extra Fire". Put "increased Energy Shield %" on rare Sapphires.
Gear priorities
The top priority is Olroth's Conviction, which lets you use Barrage 3 times on cooldown. The weapon is a high-physical-damage spear. Use 10%+ critical strike chance as the guideline for The Ordained. The sceptre's Sacred Flame is the maximum damage, but mind its synergy with Rakiata's Flow. For Trinity's balance, choose from Guiding Palm of the Eye / Guiding Palm of the Heart / Guiding Palm of the Mind. Uhtred's Sidereus and Runeforged Sekhema Sandals are also candidates.
Replacement candidates
Once you obtain Olroth's Conviction, remove Salvo from Twister. Combined with Uhtred's Constellation, Barrage becomes easier to use. Secure around 200 Ward so Barrage can be run with Olroth's Conviction.
Caution
When using Sacred Flame and Rakiata's Flow together, keep the boss away from you when using Twister (they cancel each other out). Trinity has a breakpoint at Lv23 of "+1% more elemental damage", so reach it with Uhtred's Augury or Uhtred's Omen. Garukhan's Resolve is the strongest lineage support for damage.
When to move to the next stage
You reach the stage where currency can go toward expensive finished-form gear such as Mageblood and Headhunter.
Source stage: Balance critical nodes with Evasion / Deflection nodes. Deflection nodes are brought into full use with a chest of 1500+ Evasion. Balance Trinity with Heart of the Well's "gain damage as extra Fire". Put "increased Energy Shield %" on rare Sapphires.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (Crit Aspirational)You are here
  1. Assemble the finished form's unique belt
  2. Fill in the Garukhan's Resolve and Her Declaration / Seraph's Heart lineage supports
  3. Incorporate Berserk and Ghost Dance into the final setup
Main skill
The finished Twister + Barrage form. The final setup adds Berserk and Ghost Dance.
Supports
Twister: Retreat → Acceleration → Rakiata's Flow → Garukhan's Resolve → Deliberation. Barrage: Perpetual Charge → Heightened Charges → Olroth's Conviction → Uhtred's Constellation → Efficiency II. Whirling Slash: Rapid Attacks III → Heightened Accuracy II → Blazing Critical → Pinpoint Critical → Rigwald's Ferocity. Herald of Thunder: Elemental Armament II → Concentrated Area → Blindside → Esh's Radiance → Esh's Prowess. See the source planner for the exact slots.
Passives
Take efficient flask nodes with Lavianga's Spirits. On an Evasion chest, Beastial Skin is powerful (pairs well with Slipstrike Vest and Sleek Jacket). For Controlled Metamorphosis, choose one with a small penalty to all elemental resistances (important when using Headhunter). Socket only good Time-Lost Jewels. If a Megalomaniac is a good item, take additional jewel sockets toward the Shadow / Witch side.
Gear priorities
For Mageblood, one with doubled Silver, Jade and Bismuth legacies is best. Headhunter is extremely powerful in rare-heavy content, but securing resistances and chaos resistance becomes harder. Kolr's Hunt's Marksman Gloves are a luxury item. Uhtred's Sidereus, Runeforged Sekhema Sandals, Legacy of Wanderlust and Absent Amulet are also finished-form candidates.
Replacement candidates
Her Declaration and Seraph's Heart are expensive lineage supports; before them, use Herbalism II and Clarity II. Jewels prioritize skill effect duration, ailment magnitude, critical strike chance and critical damage.
Caution
If mana problems arise, handle them with Sapphires granting 2% mana recovery on kill and a mana flask, and late on with Lavianga's Spirits. Pure Evasion or an ES / Evasion hybrid is a matter of taste; the hybrid is somewhat tankier but reduces tree freedom. The source warns that "during boss fights you are vulnerable while waiting for cooldowns".
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, progress with 3-mod jewels, max-rolling each gear piece, and strict selection of Time-Lost Jewels.

BUILD PROFILE

Twister Deadeye: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Unleashes elemental Twisters across the whole screen, with a wide clear area (source strength: Big Coverage).
  • Freezes and shatters feel satisfying, and you can map while freezing the whole screen (source strength: Satisfying Freezes and Shatters).
  • Versatile enough to handle both bosses and maps (source strength: Versatile for both bossing and Mapping).
  • It functions without uniques and is suited to league start (per the source's introduction: does not require any Uniques to function).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Squishy early on (source weakness: Squishy Early).
  • Vulnerable during boss fights while waiting for cooldowns (source weakness: Vulnerable during boss fights while waiting for cooldowns).
  • Weapon set usage is extremely important, and the build does not function if the assignments are wrong (per the source's gear explanation).
  • Leveling with Twister from Lv1 in a fresh league requires the preparation of creating a Huntress mule character first (per the source's skill priority).
  • WASD movement is recommended, and with mouse movement Whirling Slash is hard to operate (source FAQ).

Gear priorities

  1. Weapons: Weapon Set 1 is a spear prioritizing attack speed (Winged Spear / Soaring Spear, with an attack speed suffix; Skysliver is also a candidate). Weapon Set 2 is a spear with physical damage, critical strike chance and + to projectile skill levels. The off-hand is basically an Omen Sceptre, with Sacred Flame as the maximum-damage option.
  2. Gear: The top priorities are The Taming and Olroth's Conviction. Early stopgaps are Wake of Destruction and Berek's Grip / Berek's Pass / Berek's Respite, and Serpent's Egg. The finished form is Mageblood and Headhunter.
  3. Armour: Armour-focused in Acts 1–3, shifting to Evasion and Deflection from Act 3 onward, with ES / Evasion hybrid also an option late. On armour pieces, Life and resistances are the top priority, and the chest's Evasion Rating is the guideline for Deflection nodes (1200+ → 1500+). Jewellery takes added damage to attacks and mana recovery on kill.
