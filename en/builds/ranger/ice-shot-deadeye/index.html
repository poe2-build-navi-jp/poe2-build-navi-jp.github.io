Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod until Lv23. In the source's lvl 24-30, Lightning Arrow continues, and Freezing Mark, Freezing Salvo, Ice-Tipped Arrows and others are added.

Bow, quiver, Life, resistances, movement speed. Also check the required Intelligence.

Reach Lv31. Ice Shot and the post-switch supports can be prepared.

PRACTICAL GUIDE

When you're unsure about the Ice Shot switch or early damage

Fubgun's leveling guide text (title 0.5, tags 0.5.5 FR). The full dynamic support setups and actual in-game behavior have not been re-verified.

Must I always switch to Ice Shot at Lv31?

Lv31 is the guideline for starting to use it. Check reaching the level separately from having the required gems, Spirit and gear in place. If something is missing, keep the pre-switch Lightning Arrow + Lightning Rod setup.

What if damage is low before the Ice Shot switch?

The source emphasizes added attack damage on early bows, quivers and the like. First compare your current weapon, and if supports are lacking, check moving them to the boss-oriented Lightning Rod side. Do not use early DPS judgments as proof of the finished form's optimal gear.

Where do I separate the leveling and Endgame setups?

The leveling source guides you to the Endgame guide after the campaign. Do not keep using early gear as the finished form; check the current stage's supports, gear and transition conditions together.

BUILD PROFILE

Ice Shot Deadeye: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • The Lv1–30 leveling setup and the Lv31-onward Ice Shot setup are published per stage.
  • Clear-oriented Ice Shot and single-target Snipe can be given separate roles.
  • Endgame can start from a non-crit form and transition later to match your gear.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Ice Shot is not unlocked until Lv31, so early on you use Lightning Arrow and Lightning Rod.
  • Later defense and the crit transition depend on gear, and switching to the finished form too early tends to make it unstable.
  • Single-target fights require Snipe's charge and mana management.
  • Boss-oriented Snipe requires getting used to the release timing (Maxroll: "Snipe can be tricky"; Gamerch).

Gear priorities

  1. Bow and quiver: added attack damage and actual attack performance
  2. Armour pieces: Life, Evasion, elemental resistances
  3. Boots: movement speed. Later, also check required attributes and the ES transition conditions.
