LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Grenade Gemling leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 8 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

  1. Attach supports to Explosive Grenade

  3. Look for boots with movement speed
Main skill
Explosive Grenade. Frost Bomb and Flash Grenade as support.
Supports
Explosive Grenade: Multishot + Elemental Armament. Flash Grenade: Multishot.
Passives
ACT 1 stage tree. The priority nodes confirmed in writing are Demolitionist and Grenadier.
Gear priorities
Crossbow physical / elemental damage, projectile levels. Boot movement speed, added damage on gloves and rings.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize the old crossbow and boots without movement speed.
Caution
Use Explosive Grenade as the main skill, and use Frost Bomb for Exposure.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv11. You can use Explosive Grenade and the support skills by role.

Explosive Grenade + Flash Grenade. Gas Grenade once obtained.

Explosive Grenade: Multishot I, Elemental Armament I. Flash / Gas Grenade: Multishot I. Gas also takes Elemental Armament I. If using Herald of Ash, Magnified Area I and Swift Affliction I (per the source ACT 1 acquisition order).

BUILD PROFILE

Grenade Gemling: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • There is a per-stage written guide that keeps grenades as the axis from Lv1 to Endgame.
  • Explosive, Flash, Gas, Oil and Cluster Grenade can be used by situation.
  • Setups are presented separately from ACT 1 to Uber Endgame.
  • The author of the separate PoE Vault setup (0.5) explains that it can clear with the 1–2 buttons of Oil Grenade and Explosive Grenade, and can be started on a low budget.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Using multiple grenades tends to create many on-screen effects.
  • The crossbow and each slot's attack performance need to be updated in stages.
  • The Endgame version must not be partially brought into the early game; stages need to be aligned.
  • When aiming for a one-shot Cluster Grenade in the separate PoE Vault setup (0.5), sticking close to bosses and high Life regeneration to support Berserk are required. This is not a condition shared by all stages of the listed leveling route.

Gear priorities

  1. Crossbow: physical / elemental damage, projectile levels
  2. Gloves and rings: added damage, Life, resistances
  3. Armour pieces: Armour, Life, resistances, boot movement speed
