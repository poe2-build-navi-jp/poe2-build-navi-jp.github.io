BUILD PROFILE

Twister Gemling: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Once you obtain the needed gems in Act 1, the Whirling Slash and Twister combination can be your main setup.
  • Per-stage gear, skill and passive setups are published.
  • Its clear power is so high that you hardly ever get hit at all (Maxroll).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Whirling Slash is recommended to be kept at Lv1 to hold down mana consumption, so you need to be careful not to level it by mistake.
  • You need to learn the per-weapon-set skill assignments and the rotation.
  • Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the sources. Maxroll's guide also presents an optimization route that assumes uniques, so substitute gear may need consideration in SSF.
  • If it feels squishy, you need to reduce damage passives and take Evasion / Evasion-ES passives (Maxroll FAQ).

Gear priorities

  1. Early: added damage on spear, gloves and rings
  2. Boots: movement speed
  3. Armour pieces: maximum Life and resistances
  4. Weapon Set 1 (movement): prioritize attack speed, and focus on positioning with Whirling Slash.
  5. Weapon Set 2 (damage): prioritize base damage, and grow Twister's power.
  6. For defense, secure Evasion in addition to Life.
  7. The unique The Taming is a Prismatic Ring, not an amulet. When adopting it, check what you trade off against the ring slot's resistances and attributes. Follow the adopted source's stage for acquisition order; acquisition in SSF is not guaranteed.
