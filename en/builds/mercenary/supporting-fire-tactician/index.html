LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Supporting Fire Tactician leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 5 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–Mid Act 2 (crossbow leveling)You are here
  1. Set up the weapon sets (Permafrost Bolts = Set I / Fragmentation Rounds = Set II)
  2. At Lv16, craft a Varnished Crossbow and insert a Glacial Rune
  3. Sell rares and save up for the respec cost (about 21,700 gold)
Main skill
Permafrost Bolts for packs. For single target, freeze first, then Fragmentation Rounds.
Supports
Permafrost Bolts is the main skill, Frost Bomb lowers elemental resistances, and Herald of Ice raises clear speed. On bosses, apply Freezing Mark, then use Fragmentation Rounds. See the source planner for the exact support slots.
Passives
Keep crossbow nodes within an efficient range to hold down the respec cost. Rapid Reload and Adrenaline Rush help early on.
Gear priorities
Check vendor crossbows and sceptres every few levels, and buy an Iron Ring in Act 1. Movement speed on boots is mandatory. At Lv16, craft a Varnished Crossbow with a Lesser Essence of Ice or an Orb of Alchemy and insert a Glacial Rune (this carries crossbow leveling to the end). "Added damage to attacks" (cold and physical ideally) on gloves, rings and the crossbow is the top priority; the rest is Life and elemental resistances. Also pick up helmets and sceptres with "+ to Level of all Minion Skills" for later use.
Replacement candidates
The Lv16 Varnished Crossbow is effectively the final upgrade. Update flasks every 10–15 levels.
Caution
The weapon set configuration (Permafrost Bolts on Set I, Fragmentation Rounds on Set II, Crossbow Shot shared by both sets) determines early comfort. When moving between Acts or zones, a reload is occasionally needed. Avoid gambling and expensive rare purchases during the campaign so you can secure the respec cost.
When to move to the next stage
Take Unleash Hell! in mid Act 2 and unlock Supporting Fire. However, wait until Lv36 for the full transition.
Source stage: Keep crossbow nodes within an efficient range to hold down the respec cost. Rapid Reload and Adrenaline Rush help early on.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv36–Campaign End (switch to Supporting Fire)You are here
  1. Respec at Lv36 using about 21,700 gold
  2. Engrave Supporting Fire's required supports (Fire Attunement / Multishot II / Bidding I / Muster) first
  3. Line up one of each minion type (2–3 snipers)
Main skill
Use Supporting Fire on cooldown. Line up one of each minion type for Muster.
Supports
Supporting Fire: Fire Attunement, Multishot II, Bidding I and Muster are the required slots at the transition (per the source's gem engraving priority). For single target, ignite the Skeletal Sniper's command skill (Gas Arrow) with Fire Attunement. Sceptre auras prioritize Upwelling II and Clarity II. Debuffs are Elemental Weakness and Frost Bomb.
Passives
Respec at Lv36. Growing Swarm and command skill cooldown recovery are the top priorities. Take Archon of Undeath, Lord of Horrors, At Your Command, Puppet Master and Gigantic Following. Put spare attribute points into Strength first.
Gear priorities
"+ to Level of all Minion Skills" is the top priority, secured on sceptre, helmet and amulet (it grows both minion damage and durability). Strength also ties directly to damage and Life. A Shrine Sceptre grants Purity of Fire, letting you use Upwelling I and Clarity I for free, making it the early best choice.
Replacement candidates
Fully replace crossbow-oriented gear with minion skill level and Strength gear.
Caution
As a rule, field one of each minion type; only Skeletal Sniper is used at 2–3, depending on Spirit, as single-target support. The minions' own damage does not matter (except the early sniper), and you still gain Muster's bonus even if they die.
When to move to the next stage
Campaign finished, and the purchase funds (Exalted Orbs) for Enfolding Dawn are secured.
Source stage: Respec at Lv36. Growing Swarm and command skill cooldown recovery are the top priorities. Take Archon of Undeath, Lord of Horrors, At Your Command, Puppet Master and Gigantic Following. Put spare attribute points into Strength first.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early MapsYou are here
  1. Buy the Spirit-securing unique chest with top priority
  2. Buy Meginord's Girdle next
  3. Maintain Puppet Master with the Skeletal Arsonist's command
Main skill
Supporting Fire handles clear, and the Skeletal Arsonist's command skill cycles Archon of Undeath and Puppet Master.
Supports
For Supporting Fire's Bidding, choose Bidding II for a single-target lean or Bidding III for stable clear, to taste. Skeletal Brute breaks Armour with Armour Break III and also handles intimidation on bosses.
Passives
Assemble the nodes related to Archon of Undeath. Once assembled, single-target damage shifts to Supporting Fire and you can reduce Skeletal Snipers.
Gear priorities
The first purchase is Enfolding Dawn; save Exalted Orbs and buy it right away (upgrading it with forging greatly grows Armour and ES). Next, Meginord's Girdle gives a large amount of Strength and cold resistance.
Replacement candidates
Purchase in the order Enfolding Dawn → Meginord's Girdle.
Caution
If Supporting Fire is on cooldown at the moment you gain Archon of Undeath, the cooldown is not shortened retroactively. Trigger the Archon first with the Skeletal Arsonist's command, then fire.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Red Maps, with funds to upgrade to Trenchtimbre or an Omen Sceptre.
Source stage: Assemble the nodes related to Archon of Undeath. Once assembled, single-target damage shifts to Supporting Fire and you can reduce Skeletal Snipers.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Red MapsYou are here
  1. Aim for a buckler with +2 to Level of all Minion Skills
  2. Switch to an Omen Sceptre and use Malice
  3. Switch to applying Predator's Mark and Exposure with Pounce
Main skill
Supporting Fire handles both clear and bosses. Apply Predator's Mark and Cold Exposure with Pounce.
Supports
Kurgal's Leash gives Supporting Fire Unholy Might (30% of physical damage gained as chaos). Grow chaos damage with Withering Presence, and defensively layer Blasphemy + Temporal Chains, later adding Despair. Mana recovery comes from Wolf Pack's Infernal Legion I + Essence Harvest.
Passives
With Resurging Archon, remove Skeletal Sniper completely and unify both clear and single target on Supporting Fire. Add Despair with Whispers of Doom. Necrotic Touch and Grip of Evil pair well with Malice. Licking Wounds raises Life and mana recovery.
Gear priorities
A +2 Trenchtimbre (once shield resistances are no longer needed). The Omen Sceptre is the best base, and Malice greatly grows single-target damage. Secure regeneration with Emergent Instinct and stabilize mana recovery with Lavianga's Spirits. On the belt, "increased minion area of effect" and "minions deal increased damage with command skills" are mandatory-class mods.
Replacement candidates
Once the amulet and chest provide enough Spirit, graduate from Enfolding Dawn.
Caution
Use Infernal Legion I, not II, on Wolf Pack. Also, do not add Last Gasp to the Essence Harvest combination, or the harvest stops working.
When to move to the next stage
Secure an amulet that can be instilled with Dominion, and Heroic Tragedy (Vorana inscription).
Source stage: With Resurging Archon, remove Skeletal Sniper completely and unify both clear and single target on Supporting Fire. Add Despair with Whispers of Doom. Endgame (CI, Dominion obtained)You are here
  1. Instill Dominion on the amulet
  2. Move to Heroic Tragedy (Vorana inscription) + Chaos Inoculation
  3. Aim for a +4 corrupted Chober Chaber
Main skill
Stacked shots of Supporting Fire. Maintain Archon of Undeath almost permanently, handling both clear and bosses with this alone.
Supports
Vorana's Siege grows area and damage against isolated enemies. Uul-Netol's Embrace is a powerful lineage support that gains 40% of physical damage as chaos (an expensive Min-Max slot). On the minion side, finish with Amanamu's Tithe, Tecrod's Revenge, Brutus' Brain and Her Declaration.
Passives
Instilling Dominion is the top priority (Archon of Undeath's recovery period disappears, so it can be maintained almost permanently). Heroic Tragedy's Black Scythe Training converts Strength into ES, and combined with Chaos Inoculation you aim for over 10,000 ES. Hold Chober Chaber in one hand with Giant's Blood.
Gear priorities
Maximize ES on helmet, gloves and boots. Chober Chaber is a strict upgrade over Trenchtimbre; aim for a corrupted +4 to all minion skills one requiring 80–85 Intelligence. Morior Invictus requires all elemental resistances and all attributes, with reduced damage taken from critical strikes as the best third slot. Put Gigantic Following on the helmet with a Raven-Touched Shard, and another instillation on the gloves with Cadigan's Epiphany.
Replacement candidates
Fully replace Life mods with ES. At Min-Max, even Mageblood and Jiquani's Thesis come into view.
Caution
Heroic Tragedy must have the "Vorana" inscription (rolls do not matter). If you do not have a Megalomaniac with Growing Swarm, go take Growing Swarm by the normal path.
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, refine jewel mods (maximum ES, ES recharge start speed, minion critical mods) and max-roll each gear piece.

BUILD PROFILE

Supporting Fire Tactician: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Supporting Fire's base area of effect is a wide 14m, and pack clear is extremely fast (source strength: Phenomenal Clear).
  • After the transition, a one-button playstyle of firing Supporting Fire on cooldown (source strength: 1 Button Build).
  • A single stat, Strength, grows both offense (Supporting Fire's damage) and defense (Life / ES) at once (source strength: Scales Offense + Defense with One Stat).
  • The source calls it an excellent league starter, and it is also placed in A Tier in Maxroll's League Starter Tier List (source strength: Amazing League Starter).
  • The finished form reaches over 10,000 ES with Heroic Tragedy and Chaos Inoculation (per the source's gear explanation).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • You must level with a crossbow early on, and the signature skill Supporting Fire cannot be used until mid Act 2 (source weakness: Have To Play Crossbows Early On).
  • A respec cost of about 21,700 gold applies at Lv36.
  • Because you field many minions, gear needs minion mods such as minion skill levels (source weakness: Minion Tax on Gear).
  • Suffix pressure is severe in Endgame (source weakness: Suffix Pressure Endgame).

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon (sceptre): "+ to Level of all Minion Skills" and Spirit. The finished form is a corrupted Chober Chaber with +4 to minion skills.
  2. Armour: stack ES on helmet, gloves and boots. The finished form is Morior Invictus and Heroic Tragedy + Chaos Inoculation for over 10,000 ES.
  3. Strength: directly tied to Supporting Fire's damage and to Life / ES, so stack it on all gear with priority. On the belt, also secure minion area-of-effect and command skill damage mods.

FAQ

Supporting Fire Tactician build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who want to map comfortably, surrounded by a minion army while raining arrows over the whole screen.

What weakness should I know before starting?

You must level with a crossbow early on, and the signature skill Supporting Fire cannot be used until mid Act 2 (source weakness: Have To Play Crossbows Early On).
