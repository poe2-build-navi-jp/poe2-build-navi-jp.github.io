PoE2 0.5.5 Build Guides by Class

Updated: 2026-11-26

All 26 guides are checked against published sources. Each page gives you a stage-by-stage roadmap from Lv1 to Endgame: enter your current level and see the three things to do now, plus source-checked strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities.

Monk / Martial Artist

Hollow Focus Storm Wave Martial Artist

Main skill: Storm Wave

Best for: Players who want to run a fast-paced melee build that flattens maps with bell explosions and melts bosses with overlapping fissures

Weakness: Monk is the weakest of all classes early on, and Act 1 is also the Twister leveling setup's weakest point (source gear explanation).

Operation
Starts with leveling on a separate character, weapon set swapping, combo management, and positioning to overlap Storm Wave's fissures are assumed
SSF
Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source (Maxroll). The source mentions buying Skysliver and Duality and the prices of lineage supports (cheap / expensive), and much of it assumes trade

Level from Lv1

Monk / Acolyte of Chayula

Infernal Legion Acolyte of Chayula

Main skill: Attach Infernal Legion II to a companion tamed with Tame Beast (a Plague Harvester) for a radial DoT setup that constantly Ignites nearby enemies. Transfer Into the Breach's purple flames to the companion with Selfless Remnants to grow the Ignite with added chaos damage

Best for: Players who like "zero-button" play: pressing almost nothing while the area around their companion keeps burning, processing maps and bosses with DoT

Weakness: Fragile early on (source weakness: Squishy Early Game). Leveling is melee Ice Strike, not minions, and Monk's Act 1 is especially rough.

Operation
Starts with melee leveling (Ice Strike) rather than minions, and around Lv56 requires a respec of about 40,000 gold and taming two rare beasts with specified mods
SSF
Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source. The source says no unique is mandatory, but note that taming rare beasts with specified mods is assumed
Patch
0.5.5
Level from Lv1

Monk / Acolyte of Chayula

Lightning Minion Acolyte of Chayula

Main skill: A minion army setup whose main force is Spectres bound with Bind Spectre. Late in the campaign it is bomb-type Vaal Guards; from mapping, Powered Zealot / Chaos Zealot Spectres firing Spark carry the damage, and Waking Dream's purple flames are transferred to the minions with Selfless Remnants to stack added chaos damage

Best for: Players who like letting a minion army do the fighting while they focus on debuffs and support. From the Mid setup onward it runs on few buttons

Weakness: Acts 1–3 are not minion leveling (source weakness: Non-Minion leveling in Act 1-3). You progress with a crossbow and grenades, and a respec of about 35,000 gold is needed late in Act 3.

Operation
Acts 1–3 start with non-minion crossbow / grenade leveling, and late in Act 3 (around Lv41–43) a respec of about 35,000 gold is required. Spectres have different variants under the same name, so you need to identify the right type
SSF
Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source. The source says no unique is mandatory for the campaign to function, but from mapping onward much of it assumes specified uniques
Patch
0.5.5
Level from Lv1

Monk / Acolyte of Chayula

Minion Army Acolyte of Chayula

Main skill: A minion army centered on the Vaal Guards, Skeletal Reavers and Plague Harvesters bound with Bind Spectre. Into the Breach's Remnants are narrowed to added chaos damage by Choice of Power and transferred to the minions with Selfless Remnants to grow damage. Early on you level with a crossbow + Explosive Grenade and switch to minions late in Act 3

Best for: Players who like "commander" play: letting a horde of minions fight while they focus on curses and buff upkeep

Weakness: Not recommended for HC (source weakness: Not recommended for HC).

Operation
Medium to High (Acts 1–3 are grenade leveling → a respec of about 35,000 gold around Lv41–43, and identifying and binding the right Vaal Guard is required)
SSF
Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source. The source says no unique is mandatory during the campaign, but note that obtaining Expedition skill gems (Refutation, Wardbound Minions) is assumed
Patch
0.5.5
Level from Lv1

Monk / Martial Artist

Oil Barrage Martial Artist

Main skill: Oil Barrage — spends Power Charges to fire chaining, forking Nova projectiles. Generate Power Charges by using Devour on the bells of Hollow Focus or on the corpses of enemies you kill. Level with Frozen Locus + Glacial Cascade early, switch to Storm Wave from Act 2, and swap to Oil Barrage in Early Maps.

Best for: Players who like blanketing the whole screen with projectiles and cycling Power Charges recovered from bells and corpses. For Monk players who want to make the bells of Hollow Focus work.

Weakness: Built around a "don't get hit" playstyle (source weakness: Don't Get Hit Playstyle). You need evasion, positioning and killing from range.

Operation
Medium (level with a different setup through the campaign, switch to Oil Barrage in Early Maps; requires keeping Power Charges up and managing Devour on bells and corpses)
SSF
The source's strengths state "SSF Campaign ~4Hours, Arbiter ~8Hours" and the leveling guide's strengths list "SSF Fresh Start Leaguestart Viable." Reaching the campaign end in about 4 hours and the Arbiter in about 8 hours is presented as achievable in SSF.
Patch
0.5.5
Level from Lv1

Mercenary

Mercenary / Tactician

Supporting Fire Tactician

Main skill: Supporting Fire

Best for: Players who want to map comfortably, surrounded by a minion army while raining arrows over the whole screen.

Weakness: You must level with a crossbow early on, and the signature skill Supporting Fire cannot be used until mid Act 2 (source weakness: Have To Play Crossbows Early On).

Operation
There is one respec of about 21,700 gold at Lv36, but after the transition it is a one-button playstyle of firing Supporting Fire on cooldown.
SSF
Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source (Maxroll). The source states it "functions without uniques", but that is not an SSF statement, so it is treated as unconfirmed.

Sorceress

Sorceress / Disciple of Varashta

Frostbolt + Cast on Critical Comet Disciple of Varashta

Main skill: Frostbolt — Snakepit (left ring) turns its piercing into forking, and the overlapping explosions clear the screen. Shatter Frost Wall's ice crystals with Frostbolt to fire Comets in rapid succession through Cast on Elemental Ailment / Cast on Critical. Before you have Snakepit, level with Ice Nova (clear) and Frost Darts (single target), and switch to Frostbolt once you can equip Snakepit at Lv32. The Disciple of Varashta ascendancy Instruments of Power lets you use a staff and focus together, and Baryanic Leylines widens the radius of Time-Lost Sapphires.

Best for: Players who like freezing enemies in place while controlling the screen with Frostbolt explosions and automatically falling Comets. For players who like a Disciple of Varashta build that uses a staff + focus through Instruments of Power and makes use of Time-Lost Sapphires.

Weakness: Frostbolt cannot be used early (source weakness: Can't use it early). Level with Ice Nova + Frost Darts until you can equip Snakepit at Lv32.

Operation
Medium (level through the campaign with a separate Ice Nova + Frost Darts setup, switch to Frostbolt after equipping Snakepit at Lv32; requires managing Infusion Remnants and shattering Frost Wall crystals)
SSF
The source does not state SSF. Because Snakepit is mandatory, SSF is treated as unconfirmed and set to null.
Patch
0.5.5
Level from Lv1

Minion Spirit Walker

Main skill: An army of unique beasts captured with Tame Beast, the Wild Protector bear spirit, Wolf Pack and Skeletal minions

Weakness: The main army setup cannot be used from the start. You level with a crossbow (grenades) until Lv22, and need gold for the passive respec (U4GM summary / Zizaran's video summary).

Operation
Level with a crossbow early, respec into the minion setup at Lv22. Requires companion selection and Spirit management

Ranger / Deadeye

Glacial Lance Deadeye

Main skill: Glacial Lance

Best for: Players who want to map at high speed alongside mirages while freezing the whole screen.

Weakness: It is not designed to keep taking boss attacks head-on (source weakness: Not Made To Facetank Bosses).

Operation
Twister leveling until the first trial in Act 2. After the transition, there is timing management for the two mirages and Barrage.
SSF
The source (Maxroll) states it "functions without uniques", but does not state whether it works in SSF. It also contains trade-assuming descriptions such as buying the Idol of Eramir and instillations on amulets, so it is not treated as SSF-confirmed.
