LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Infernal Legion Acolyte of Chayula leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–13 (Act 1 early: Quarterstaff)You are here
  1. Upgrade to a high-damage Quarterstaff
  2. Prioritize maximum Life and resistances on gear
  3. Replace flasks with the newest ones
Main skill
Progress with a high-damage Quarterstaff. Break Frozen Locus ice crystals with the tip of Glacial Cascade to cover both pack clearing and single target
Supports
The policy is to supplement the Quarterstaff's damage with runes such as a Lesser Glacial Rune (source).
Passives
Lay the groundwork toward Concussive Attack. Ideally take Hollow Palm Technique by the Count Geonor fight in Act 1 (source).
Gear priorities
A Quarterstaff with high physical / cold damage. For the remaining slots, prioritize maximum Life and resistances. Evasion or Evasion / ES bases are preferable, but mod content matters more than the base (source).
Replacement candidates
Update flasks to the newest ones every 10–15 levels (source).
Caution
The source calls the early part of Monk's Act 1 a rough section. Do not overextend until Ice Strike unlocks.
When to move to the next stage
Ice Strike unlocks at Lv14.

Lv14–End of Act 4 (Ice Strike leveling)You are here
  1. Build freeze chains with Ice Strike and Herald of Ice
  2. Save about 40,000 gold for the respec
  3. Secure minion gear (sceptre, helmet) when you see it
Main skill
Ice Strike is the main skill. Explode frozen enemies with Herald of Ice, and dive into large packs with Shattering Palm. On bosses, place Tempest Bell and cycle the combo
Supports
Embitter is important: it converts Into the Breach's added chaos damage to cold so you can take Freeze (source). Cannibalism I and Clarity I supplement Life / Mana recovery. Putting Freezing Mark and Elemental Weakness on Hand of Chayula lets you apply both with one button (source).
Passives
Taking Hollow Palm Technique lets you fight without a weapon and frees you from hunting for melee weapons. Remnant Attraction makes collecting Flames of Chayula easier. The first ascendancy point is Waking Dream (source).
Gear priorities
+levels to melee skills on gloves and amulet is the damage core. Balance Life and resistances. During the campaign, if you see minion gear for after the transition (a sceptre, or a helmet with +levels to all minion skills), secure it (source).
Replacement candidates
After taking Hollow Palm Technique, you no longer need to update your weapon. If you have the funds, Thrillsteel and Yoke of Suffering are candidates (source).
Caution
This stage is not a minion setup yet. To secure the respec cost (about 39,000–40,000 gold), pick up and sell rares, and do not buy expensive rares from vendors (source). Early defense is fragile — that is a weakness in the source.
When to move to the next stage
Finish Act 4 and enter the Interludes. Lv56+ and 18+ weapon set points are the guide for the transition.
Source stage: Taking Hollow Palm Technique lets you fight without a weapon and frees you from hunting for melee weapons. Remnant Attraction makes collecting Flames of Chayula easier. The first ascendancy point is Waking Dream (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Around Lv56–Transition (tame companions and respec)You are here
  1. Tame a Plague Harvester (all damage can Ignite)
  2. Tame a Diretusk Boar (all damage can Shock)
  3. Respec your passives for about 40,000 gold
Main skill
Tame the two companions first, then respec your passives and transition to the Infernal Legion II setup. The main companion is a Plague Harvester with the "all damage can Ignite" mod; the support companion is a Diretusk Boar with the "all damage can Shock" mod (source)
Supports
Cut Infernal Legion II with a Lv5 Uncut Support Gem. Do not use Infernal Legion I — its fire resistance and area are insufficient (stated in the source). Put Selfless Remnants on Into the Breach to transfer the purple flame's effect to allies in your Presence. After taking Gigantic Following, replace Hulking Minions with Searing Flame II (source).
Passives
The respec costs about 40,000 gold. Vile Mending, Living Death, minion fire resistance and Unspoken Bond are required nodes so your companions do not kill themselves with Infernal Legion (source). Long Distance Relationship and Bond of the Ape extend the companion's area of effect. The ascendancy order is Waking Dream → Choice of Power → Chayula's Gift (source).
Gear priorities
A sceptre, helmet and amulet with +levels to all minion skills. A Shrine Sceptre is the best base, but mod content matters more than the base (source).
Replacement candidates
Prepare Saitha's Spear in Weapon Set II (to Aggravate boss Ignites). Set both companions to Weapon Set I only, so a weapon swap can instantly heal and recall them (source).
Caution
Finish the taming before the respec. The Plague Harvester is around Khari Crossing in the Interludes, and the Diretusk Boar spawns fixed at the Troubled Camp in Act 3's Infested Barrens, where you can reroll its mods with checkpoint respawns (source FAQ). Be careful not to kill the rare beasts.
When to move to the next stage
Both companions are tamed, the respec is done, and you can enter maps with Infernal Legion II.

Early MappingYou are here
  1. Obtain a Buckler with +1 to all minion skill levels
  2. Make a habit of entering zones with Weapon Set II
  3. On bosses, follow the order: Ignite → Frost Bomb → Spearfield
Main skill
Process maps with the companion's radial DoT. On entering a zone, enter with Weapon Set II (the Into the Breach snapshot), and apply Exposure with Frost Bomb. On bosses, have the companion apply Ignite, then weapon swap and Aggravate the Ignite with Spearfield (source)
Supports
With a Shrine Sceptre, you can use Spirit-reservation supports such as Upwelling II effectively for free. Put leftover Spirit into Ghost Dance. Enable Withering Presence only after obtaining Blackflame (source).
Passives
The stage where you use Mind Over Matter to use Mana as a mitigation pool (source). On jewels, prioritize increased Presence area, aiming for a radius of about 7m (source).
Gear priorities
Obtain Trenchtimbre's +1 roll early and replace the Buckler. Using Blackflame requires +92 or more additional Spirit from gear. Saitha's Spear is cheap and powerful (source).
Replacement candidates
For the sceptre, prioritize mods. If a Shrine Sceptre is expensive, you may save with a Stoic Sceptre or similar (source).
Caution
If a companion leaves your Presence, your maximum ES drops visibly. If it happens often, add more increased Presence area jewels (source).
When to move to the next stage
Enter the red map range, where you can aim for Trenchtimbre's +2 roll and Genesis Tree minion gear.
Source stage: The stage where you use Mind Over Matter to use Mana as a mitigation pool (source). On jewels, prioritize increased Presence area, aiming for a radius of about 7m (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Red Maps (transition to the Wasp setup)You are here
  1. Obtain Xoph's Pyre and transition to the Wasp setup
  2. Update Trenchtimbre to the +2 roll
  3. Assemble increased minion area on jewels
Main skill
Transition to the Wasp setup, which assumes Xoph's Pyre. The mains are a Plague Harvester with Shroud Walker and a Gargantuan Wasp, which teleport to enemies while spreading area Ignites. Put Catha's Brilliance on the other companion instead (source)
Supports
Scouring Flame is a strict upgrade over Searing Flame II. A Skeletal Cleric helps companions survive. Charged Mark and Sniper's Mark create shocked ground (source). Convalescence is insurance that starts ES recharge immediately when you take damage.
Passives
The top jewel priority is "increased minion area of effect" (only rolls on Ruby and Diamond). Next, increased Presence area and minion maximum Life (source).
Gear priorities
Update Trenchtimbre to the +2 roll. Replace belt and rings with Genesis Tree minion pieces; the belt's "increased minion area" is the most important. Replace Blackflame with minion rings one by one (source).
Replacement candidates
If resistances are short, supplement with runes. Instilling Warlord Leader on the amulet extends the Presence radius and damage (source).
Caution
Catha's Brilliance increases the companion's Spirit cost. If you come up short, take additional companion reservation nodes; if it is still not enough, go back to Infernal Legion II (source). The Elephant Tortoise setup deals about 80% more damage, but it requires AI herding (holding Direct Minions), and the source treats it as an alternative.
When to move to the next stage
ES reaches about 6,000 and you can transition to Chaos Inoculation.
Source stage: The top jewel priority is "increased minion area of effect" (only rolls on Ruby and Diamond). Next, increased Presence area and minion maximum Life (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (Finished) (Chaos Inoculation form)You are here
  1. Transition to Chaos Inoculation at about 6,000 ES
  2. Update the sceptre to a Shrine Sceptre with Purity of Fire
  3. Keep rerolling companion mods
Main skill
After taking Chaos Inoculation, run with about 10,000 ES and over 50% Evasion. The companion's area Ignite is constant DPS, and you focus on Frost Bomb and debuff upkeep (source)
Supports
At this stage the sceptre is assumed to be a Shrine Sceptre with Purity of Fire. If you cannot hold one, take Living Death in the passives (source).
Passives
Add notables with Megalomaniac and Prism of Belief jewels. Defense and companion Life nodes also raise ES, Evasion and damage at the same time (source).
Gear priorities
Maximize ES on helmet, chest and focus (focus: +2 to all minion skill levels). Sacrificial Regalia, Jiquani's Thesis and Mageblood are finished-form candidates. Add Transcendent Limbs only after you finish rerolling your ideal companion (source).
Replacement candidates
Maximum Life and chaos resistance rolls become unnecessary after Chaos Inoculation. Update one slot at a time based on ES and minion stats (source).
Caution
A large part of your ES and Evasion depends on the companion being inside your Presence (source). There is no useful fourth ascendancy notable, so the fourth ascendancy is not urgent (stated in the source).
Completion condition
After finishing, progress by rerolling companion mods (Shroud Walker + all damage can Ignite, etc.) and updating jewels.

BUILD PROFILE

Infernal Legion Acolyte of Chayula: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • High damage. Into the Breach's added chaos damage reaches the 190% range at maximum, and combined with an "all damage can Ignite" companion, Ignite damage nearly triples (source).
  • Mapping is "zero-button". A constant radial DoT keeps occurring around the companion (source strength: Constant Radial DoT DPS).
  • The finished form's defense is very hard: about 10,000 ES, over 50% Evasion, and Chaos Inoculation gives full immunity to chaos damage, bleeding and poison (source strength: Very Tanky Endgame).
  • Bosses melt in seconds with Frost Bomb and Saitha's Spear's Spearfield (source summary).
  • No unique is mandatory for it to function. Cheap options such as Blackflame and Trenchtimbre can grow the early game (source).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Fragile early on (source weakness: Squishy Early Game). Leveling is melee Ice Strike, not minions, and Monk's Act 1 is especially rough.
  • Leveling is not a minion setup (source weakness: Non-Minion Leveling). Around Lv56, a respec of about 40,000 gold is required.
  • You must tame companions with specified mods (source weakness: Must Tame Specific Companion). A Plague Harvester (all damage can Ignite) and a Diretusk Boar (all damage can Shock) are the minimum requirement.
  • If you do not make the Weapon Set II snapshot on zone entry a habit, the Remnant pickup area stays narrow (source FAQ).
  • Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source.

Gear priorities

  1. +levels to all minion skills: secure it on the sceptre, helmet and amulet. The top-priority stat, affecting both the companion's damage and durability (source).
  2. Spirit: +92 or more additional Spirit from gear is the requirement to use Blackflame. With a Shrine Sceptre, you can use Spirit-reservation supports effectively for free (source).
  3. Trenchtimbre: early on the +1 roll, updated to the +2 roll in red maps. Cheap and a large gain (source).
  4. Companion maximum Life and increased minion area of effect: Infernal Legion II's base damage scales with the companion's maximum Life, and increased area translates directly into clear area (source).
  5. Defense: build on Evasion / ES bases, and transition to Chaos Inoculation at about 6,000 ES. The finished form is about 10,000 ES + over 50% Evasion (source).
  6. Jewels: prioritize "increased minion area of effect" (Ruby / Diamond), increased Presence area, and minion maximum Life (source).

FAQ

Infernal Legion Acolyte of Chayula build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who like "zero-button" play: pressing almost nothing while the area around their companion keeps burning, processing maps and bosses with DoT

What weakness should I know before starting?

Fragile early on (source weakness: Squishy Early Game). Leveling is melee Ice Strike, not minions, and Monk's Act 1 is especially rough.
