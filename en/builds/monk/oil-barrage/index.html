PoE2 Oil Barrage Martial Artist Build | 0.5.5 Leveling Guide

Updated: 2026-11-26

Monk / Martial Artist

Main skill: Oil Barrage — spends Power Charges to fire chaining, forking Nova projectiles. Generate Power Charges by using Devour on the bells of Hollow Focus or on the corpses of enemies you kill. Level with Frozen Locus + Glacial Cascade early, switch to Storm Wave from Act 2, and swap to Oil Barrage in Early Maps.

LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Oil Barrage Martial Artist leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–13 (Hollow Palm leveling, Act 1 first half)You are here
  1. Place Frozen Locus and detonate it with Glacial Cascade's big spike
  2. Take Hollow Palm Technique at Lv13
  3. Get boots with 10%+ movement speed and armor pieces with plenty of Life
Main skill
Progress with a Quarterstaff, place Frozen Locus from Lv2 and detonate it with the big final spike of Glacial Cascade. Add Pounce at Lv6 for movement and Herald of Ice at Lv10. At Lv13 take Hollow Palm Technique and the Quarterstaff is no longer needed.
Supports
Glacial Cascade: Concentrated Area + Rage I. Frozen Locus: Ice Bite. Herald of Ice: Magnified Area.
Passives
Rush Hollow Palm Technique as fast as possible. Take efficient early damage nodes on the way, and enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements (source).
Gear priorities
A high-DPS magic / rare Quarterstaff. Boots with 10%+ movement speed. Helmet, chest and belt with plenty of Life. You may socket the weapon with an Artificer's Orb and reinforce it with Lesser Runes (source).
Replacement candidates
Once you take Hollow Palm Technique at Lv13, the Quarterstaff no longer needs upgrading. Only equip pieces that raise DPS substantially (source).
Caution
Most of Glacial Cascade's damage is in its final big spike. To break Frozen Locus in one hit, land that spike. Consuming Freeze adds a 250% or 425% extra damage multiplier (source).
When to move to the next stage
Take Hollow Palm Technique at Lv13 and continue through Act 1.
Source stage: Rush Hollow Palm Technique as fast as possible. Take efficient early damage nodes on the way, and enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv14–Campaign End (Storm Wave leveling)You are here
  1. Clear by detonating Frozen Locus with Storm Wave
  2. Add Herald of Thunder to speed up clearing
  3. Save the Greater Jeweller's Orb for Oil Barrage
Main skill
Switch to Storm Wave in Act 2 (around Lv22) and clear by detonating Frozen Locus. For single target, freeze with Wave of Frost and consume the Freeze with Glacial Cascade's big spike for heavy damage. Add Herald of Thunder in Act 3.
Supports
Storm Wave: Rage II + Elemental Armament II + Branching Fissures. Glacial Cascade: Concentrated Area + Elemental Armament II + Elemental Focus. Frozen Locus: Ice Bite II + Elemental Armament II + Innervate. Frost Bomb: Potent Exposure. Herald of Ice: Magnified Area II + Elemental Armament II. Hand of Chayula: Freezing Mark + Elemental Weakness. Wave of Frost: Deep Freeze + Ice Bite II. Pounce: Cooldown Recovery II.
Passives
Start allocating Weapon Set 2 points (for Pounce). First ascendancy: Way of the Mountain → Hollow Focus Technique. Late in the campaign there are respecs into strong scaling nodes for the move to maps (source note).
Gear priorities
Gloves and amulet with +levels to melee skills, rings with flat elemental damage to attacks. Prioritize Life and resistances. Boots with 20–30% movement speed. On trade, Horns of Bynden and Luminous Pace are early options (source).
Replacement candidates
Save the Greater Jeweller's Orb guaranteed in Act 4 for Oil Barrage (strong instruction in the source).
Caution
There are several passive respecs late in the campaign. If you are short on gold, sell rare items to save up (source).
When to move to the next stage
Clear the campaign and enter Early Maps. Prepare a Spiny Talisman with 300+ elemental DPS.
Source stage: Start allocating Weapon Set 2 points (for Pounce). First ascendancy: Way of the Mountain → Hollow Focus Technique. Late in the campaign there are respecs into strong scaling nodes for the move to maps (source note).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early Maps (switch to Oil Barrage)You are here
  1. Devour a bell to gain Power Charges
  2. Prepare a talisman with 300+ elemental DPS
  3. Socket Nova Projectiles I into Oil Barrage
Main skill
Switch to Oil Barrage. At the start of a map, Devour a Hollow Focus bell to gain your first Power Charge, then clear with Oil Barrage. Regularly Devour bells and the corpses of enemies you kill to keep Power Charges up. Place Tempest Bell with Whirling Assault to create more chain / fork starting points.
Supports
Oil Barrage: Ricochet II + Fork + Salvo + Nova Projectiles I. Rend: Heightened Charges + Prolonged Duration II + Rage III. Hollow Focus: Cooldown Recovery II + Magnified Area II + Overabundance II. Devour: Blazing Critical + Thrill of the Kill II + Charge Profusion II + Rage III. Tempest Bell: Ancestral Call II + Magnified Area II + Delayed Gratification. Whirling Assault: Practiced Combo + Rage III + Rapid Attacks III. Hand of Chayula: Freezing Mark + Elemental Weakness + Mark of Siphoning II. Refutation: Cooldown Recovery II + Mobility + Efficiency II. Wind Dancer: Magnified Area II + Stun III + Enduring Impact II. Ghost Dance: Cooldown Recovery II + Clarity. Use Charge Regulation as a persistent buff.
Passives
Third ascendancy: Way of the Stonefist. Weapon Set 1 takes attack nodes; Weapon Set 2 takes parry nodes that strengthen Refutation (source).
Gear priorities
A Spiny Talisman with 300+ elemental DPS (guide price: 5–20 Exalted plus 10–20 Exalted of additional investment). Basic gear at 1–2 Exalted per slot. After taking Way of the Stonefist, choose gloves by the mod priority (critical damage → base critical strike chance → attack speed → Onslaught on hit) (source).
Replacement candidates
Craft the talisman yourself by identifying rares and using Transmutation / Augmentation / Regal Orbs, or buy it on trade (source). Uncut Skill Gems (Lv14) and Uncut Support Gems (Lv5) drop more often in maps.
Caution
Always use Nova Projectiles I. With II socketed, the other gems no longer all fit (strong warning in the source). While your critical strike chance is low, you can support Power Charge generation with a Blasphemy + Repulsion + Ailith's Chimes setup (at the cost of the Charge Regulation and Ghost Dance slots).
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv77+ and be able to upgrade the talisman to 380+ elemental DPS. Anoint the amulet with Thaumaturgic Generator.
Source stage: Third ascendancy: Way of the Stonefist. Weapon Set 1 takes attack nodes; Weapon Set 2 takes parry nodes that strengthen Refutation (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv77–Late Maps (stronger talisman + defense)You are here
  1. Anoint the amulet with Thaumaturgic Generator
  2. At 50%+ critical strike chance, swap Pinpoint Critical for Elemental Armament II
  3. Raise survivability with a chest piece that summons a guardian (Forgotten Warden)
Main skill
Oil Barrage stays the core skill. Once your critical strike chance reaches 50%+, swap Pinpoint Critical for Elemental Armament II. Put Oil Barrage into Spirit Vessel to raise single-target damage.
Supports
On top of the Early Maps setup: Oil Barrage takes Pinpoint Critical (while below 50%; swap to Elemental Armament II at 50%+). Rend adds Perpetual Charge. Hollow Focus adds Prolonged Duration II. Hand of Chayula adds Charged Mark. Refutation adds Prolonged Duration II. Spirit Vessel: Oil Barrage + Potent Exposure + Hulking Minions + Romira's Requital + Amanamu's Tithe.
Passives
Fourth ascendancy: Runic Meridians. Fill in critical nodes and raise your critical strike chance. On Time-Lost Jewels prioritize critical strike chance and critical damage (1–2 mods; radius does not matter at this stage) (source).
Gear priorities
A talisman with 380+ elemental DPS and +levels to melee / attack skills (10–50 Exalted, optionally 1–2 Divine). Upgrade skill gems with Uncut Skill Gems (Lv17–18). Anoint the amulet with Thaumaturgic Generator. The Forgotten Warden raises survivability substantially. Time-Lost Jewel (critical strike chance and critical damage first) (source).
Replacement candidates
Upgrade each slot to the 5–10 Exalted range. Completing the third and fourth ascendancies is a large power gain, but you may wait until you feel confident (source).
Caution
Unless you convert Fists of Stone into Runeforged Fists of Stone with a Versium Anvil, Refutation cannot be used in the Forgotten Warden's Spirit Vessel setup (source). Do not use Refutation while clearing (you would take heavy stuns).
When to move to the next stage
Move to Endgame once the talisman reaches 490+ elemental DPS with 15%+ critical strike chance, item rarity approaches 150%, and gems are at Lv19–20 with 20% quality.
Source stage: Fourth ascendancy: Runic Meridians. Fill in critical nodes and raise your critical strike chance. On Time-Lost Jewels prioritize critical strike chance and critical damage (1–2 mods; radius does not matter at this stage) (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (100% crit, high item rarity)You are here
  1. Bring critical strike chance close to 100%
  2. Aim for 150% item rarity
  3. Upgrade the talisman to 490+ elemental DPS
Main skill
Oil Barrage (Lv20, 20% quality). Power Charge generation becomes increasingly automatic: Thaumaturgic Generator + Way of the Stonefist gloves (Power Charge on critical strike) + Ailith's Chimes on Hollow Focus. On bosses, place Tempest Bell with Whirling Assault to raise single-target damage.
Supports
Oil Barrage: Elemental Armament II (Pinpoint Critical removed). Hollow Focus: Living Lightning II + Culmination II + Ailith's Chimes + Charge Profusion II. Rend adds Rapid Attacks III. Hand of Chayula adds Mark for Death II. Refutation adds Rapid Casting II. Wind Dancer adds Blind II. Spirit Vessel stays as in Late Maps.
Passives
Bring critical strike chance close to 100%. Balance Energy Shield with Evasion and Deflect. Fill in area-of-effect jewels (3–4 mod Time-Lost Jewels prioritizing critical strike chance and critical damage, and suffix-effect jewels) (source).
Gear priorities
A talisman with 490+ elemental DPS, 15%+ critical strike chance and +skill levels. Aim for 150% item rarity. Boots with 35%+ movement speed, Evasion and Deflect. Upgrade the Forgotten Warden to better rolls. Gems at Lv19–20 with 20% quality (source).
Replacement candidates
Whenever you replace the amulet, always re-anoint Thaumaturgic Generator (strong warning in the source).
Caution
The channeling delay is resolved later by skill speed (source weakness). The build assumes a "don't get hit" playstyle, and some enemies such as Abyss monsters need high damage and area coverage (source FAQ).
When to move to the next stage
Move to Aspirational once you are ready for long-term goals such as Lv21 gems, Rakiata's Flow and Zarokh's Gift.
Source stage: Bring critical strike chance close to 100%. Balance Energy Shield with Evasion and Deflect. Fill in area-of-effect jewels (3–4 mod Time-Lost Jewels prioritizing critical strike chance and critical damage, and suffix-effect jewels) (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Aspirational (Lv21 gems, long-term goals)You are here
  1. Raise the main skill to Lv21
  2. Switch to the Rakiata's Flow setup
  3. Use separate setups for mapping and for bosses
Main skill
Oil Barrage Lv21, 0% quality (when using Rakiata's Flow). Remove Elemental Weakness from Hand of Chayula and remove Oil Barrage from Spirit Vessel (instructions for the Rakiata's Flow setup in the source). You may use Hand of Chayula instead of Pounce. For mapping there is an option to rebuild into a Blasphemy + Repulsion setup.
Supports
Oil Barrage: Rakiata's Flow. Hand of Chayula adds Eternal Mark. Charge Regulation adds Uhtred's Exodus. For mapping, Blasphemy takes Repulsion + Living Lightning II + Culmination II + Ailith's Chimes + Charge Profusion II.
Passives
Fill critical strike chance and critical damage with 5-mod jewels and 4–5 mod Time-Lost Jewels. Raise jewels to 20% quality (source).
Gear priorities
The highest elemental DPS (or physical DPS) talisman you can buy (15%+ critical strike chance, attack speed, +skill levels). Put a Raven-Touched Shard on the helmet and upgrade the amulet anoint from Thaumaturgic Generator to Zarokh's Gift. Keep item rarity at 150%+. Rite of Passage spirits, in priority order: Spirit of the Stag (movement speed, skill speed, added lightning damage) first, then Spirit of the Cat (critical strike chance and evasion), Spirit of the Wolf (movement speed, skill speed, armour break), Spirit of the Owl (Energy Shield and damage), Spirit of the Bear (maximum Life and damage reduction) (source priority order).
Replacement candidates
Many talismans look higher-DPS because of a Greater Iron Rune; when buying, be careful of numbers that include the rune (source note).
Caution
Aspirational gear is a long-term goal collected over most of a league (source). The spirits are given in English here because their Japanese names could not be confirmed on poe2db.
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, progress by adding more mods to jewels, max-rolling each gear piece, and refining the Time-Lost Jewel.
Source stage: Fill critical strike chance and critical damage with 5-mod jewels and 4–5 mod Time-Lost Jewels. Raise jewels to 20% quality (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source

BUILD PROFILE

Oil Barrage Martial Artist: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • A league starter that reaches the campaign end in about 4 hours and the Arbiter in about 8 hours even in SSF (listed as a strength in the source).
  • Very large single-target damage (source strength: Big Single Target Damage). The source calls Oil Barrage "tremendous single target damage, which is easy to scale."
  • Screen-wide clear (source strength: Screen-Wide Clear). Chaining, forking Nova projectiles cover the whole screen.
  • Layered defenses (source strength: Layered Defences). A large ES pool + Evasion + Deflect + Wind Dancer + Ghost Dance.
  • No unique is mandatory (source). The Forgotten Warden is a useful defensive upgrade while progressing, but it is not required.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Built around a "don't get hit" playstyle (source weakness: Don't Get Hit Playstyle). You need evasion, positioning and killing from range.
  • There is a channeling delay (source weakness). Skill speed resolves it later on.
  • The main skill, Oil Barrage, is not usable until Early Maps (source). The campaign is played with a different Frozen Locus + Storm Wave setup.
  • The swap needs skill gems, passive / ascendancy respec gold, and a 300+ elemental DPS talisman (source).
  • Power Charges must be maintained (source). Damage falls off if you do not regularly Devour bells and corpses. Generation is unstable early while your critical strike chance is low.

Gear priorities

  1. Talisman: elemental DPS first. Aim for 300+ in Early Maps, 380+ from Lv77, 490+ in Endgame, with 15%+ critical strike chance and +skill levels (source).
  2. Power Charge generation: anoint the amulet with Thaumaturgic Generator, Way of the Stonefist gloves (Power Charge on critical strike), and Ailith's Chimes on Hollow Focus (source).
  3. Defense: a large ES pool + Evasion + Deflect. Prefer ES / Evasion bases, and use the Forgotten Warden's Spirit Vessel to reinforce defense and damage (source).
  4. Critical: bring Oil Barrage's critical strike chance close to 100%. On Time-Lost Jewels, prioritize critical strike chance and critical damage (source).
  5. Item rarity: aim for 100% → 150%. The upgrade rate to rare currency rises sharply (source FAQ).
  6. Gloves (Way of the Stonefist): prioritize mods in the order critical damage → base critical strike chance → attack speed → Onslaught on hit (source).
  7. Movement speed: boots at 10%+ early, 20–30% during the campaign, 35%+ in Endgame (source).

FAQ

Oil Barrage Martial Artist build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who like blanketing the whole screen with projectiles and cycling Power Charges recovered from bells and corpses. For Monk players who want to make the bells of Hollow Focus work.

What weakness should I know before starting?

Built around a "don't get hit" playstyle (source weakness: Don't Get Hit Playstyle). You need evasion, positioning and killing from range.
