LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Whirling Assault Martial Artist leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 8 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–10You are here
  1. Upgrade Glacial Cascade as the top priority
  2. Place Frozen Locus, then break it with Glacial Cascade
  3. Look for boots with movement speed
Main skill
Glacial Cascade + Frozen Locus
Supports
Glacial Cascade: Concentrated Area → Cold Attunement. Frozen Locus: Ice Bite I → Cold Attunement.
Passives
Follow the staged "Glacial 1-22" tree. Individual node names cannot be extracted safely, so check the pickup order in the source tree below.
Gear priorities
On the weapon, prioritize increased physical damage and added damage. Boots: movement speed. Armor: resistances, Life, Evasion.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize replacing a weapon whose damage is not growing, and boots without movement speed.
Caution
Place Frozen Locus on enemy packs and break it with Glacial Cascade from behind. Add Herald of Ice at Lv10.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv11, with the basic Frozen Locus + Glacial Cascade combo stable.
Source stage: Follow the staged "Glacial 1-22" tree. Individual node names cannot be extracted safely, so check the pickup order in the source tree below.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv11–20You are here
  1. Keep updating Glacial Cascade first
  2. Apply Shock with Tempest Flurry only in boss fights
  3. Secure armor with resistances and Life
Main skill
Glacial Cascade + Frozen Locus. From Lv14, add Tempest Flurry (once its requirements are met) to apply Shock on bosses.
Supports
Continue the early setup. Put Shock on Tempest Flurry and use it to apply Shock to bosses.
Passives
Continue the staged "Glacial 1-22" tree. Do not stray from the cold / attack pathing.
Gear priorities
Weapon: increased physical and added damage. Boots: movement speed. Everything else: resistances, Life, Evasion.
Replacement candidates
Replace weapons that are far below your current level, and armor pieces lacking defenses or resistances.
Caution
Tempest Flurry is not the main skill for regular clearing; use it to apply Shock to bosses.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv21, and be able to reproduce the Frozen Locus + Glacial Cascade combo in boss fights.
Source stage: Continue the staged "Glacial 1-22" tree. Do not stray from the cold / attack pathing.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv21–30You are here
  1. Decide at Lv22 whether to move to Storm Wave
  2. Check the gear requirements before adopting Hollow Palm
  3. Do not sell your weapon right after switching
Main skill
Glacial Cascade until Lv22. From Lv22 you can move to the Storm Wave route.
Supports
For Storm Wave's support setup, use the "Stormwave 22+" stage of the 0.5.5 guide. For slots whose names cannot be confirmed, see the source guide.
Passives
Switch to the staged "Stormwave 22+" tree. Take Hollow Palm Technique only if you understand its gear requirements.
Gear priorities
If you adopt Hollow Palm, remove the weapon. Otherwise keep using a strong Quarterstaff.
Replacement candidates
Before switching to Hollow Palm, assemble Evasion / Energy Shield gear that keeps your damage working without a weapon.
Caution
Hollow Palm is not automatically optimal; a good Quarterstaff can deal more damage.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv31, and decide between the Storm Wave setup and continuing with Glacial Cascade.
Source stage: Switch to the staged "Stormwave 22+" tree. Take Hollow Palm Technique only if you understand its gear requirements.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv31–40You are here
  1. Prepare Whirling Assault for Lv41
  2. Check the required Dexterity and Intelligence
  3. Keep your pre-switch main skill in place
Main skill
Storm Wave, or keep Glacial Cascade if it is stable.
Supports
Maintain the staged setup of your chosen leveling route, and prepare the gems for Lv41.
Passives
Progress so you can connect into the staged "Lv41+ Whirling" tree. Check individual nodes in the source tree.
Gear priorities
Prioritize resistances, Life and Evasion, and also check the Dexterity / Intelligence requirements after the Lv41 switch.
Replacement candidates
Replace in advance any slots that would not meet gear requirements after the Lv41 switch.
Caution
Whirling Assault unlocks at Lv41. Do not remove your current main skill early.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv41, have Whirling Assault ready, and meet the required attributes.
Source stage: Progress so you can connect into the staged "Lv41+ Whirling" tree. Check individual nodes in the source tree.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv41–Campaign EndYou are here
  1. Switch to Whirling Assault
  2. Check mana and support requirements in the leveling setup
  3. Set up Tempest Bell and Mantra of Destruction for bosses
Main skill
Whirling Assault as the main skill, combined with Staggering Palm, Tempest Bell and Mantra of Destruction.
Supports
The source's leveling tab lists Magnified Area II, Rage II, Heavy Swing and Pursuit II. Adding Pursuit II is presented as the priority use for a Greater Jeweller's Orb. Add supports only once slots, owned gems and attribute requirements are met.
Passives
Switch to the "Leveling Whirling 41+" tree of the 0.5.5 guide.
Gear priorities
Quarterstaff: added damage, increased damage, +levels to melee / attack skills, critical, attack speed. Armor: resistances, Life, Evasion.
Replacement candidates
If a good Quarterstaff outperforms Hollow Palm, go back to the weapon setup.
Caution
The leveling tab presents mana options: Conservative Casting, Mana Leech, or Pounce with Mark of Siphoning. Choose after checking your owned gems and attribute requirements.
When to move to the next stage
Finish the campaign. You can clear with Whirling Assault and use Staggering Palm, Tempest Bell and Mantra of Destruction as the situation calls for.
Source stage: Switch to the "Leveling Whirling 41+" tree of the 0.5.5 guide.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Start of MappingYou are here
  1. Make the first charge with Killing Palm
  2. Keep Charged Staff up
  3. Use Falling Thunder after Whirling Assault
Main skill
Killing Palm → Charged Staff → Whirling Assault → Falling Thunder.
Supports
In the source's "Early Endgame Whirling", check the supports that fit your owned gems and slot count. Do not assume the Late Endgame 5-link setup or the high-tier Falling Thunder supports are available at the start of mapping.
Passives
Move to the "Early Endgame Whirling" tree. For the ascendancy, check the current guide, which includes Way of the Stonefist.
Gear priorities
First stabilize resistances and defenses, then grow the Quarterstaff's damage, melee skill levels, critical and attack speed.
Replacement candidates
Check in order: armor lacking resistances, boots lacking movement speed, a Quarterstaff whose damage has stalled.
Caution
At the start of a map, make your first Power Charge with Killing Palm before activating Charged Staff.
When to move to the next stage
You can keep Charged Staff from dropping, and chain into Falling Thunder after roughly two Whirling Assaults.
Source stage: Move to the "Early Endgame Whirling" tree. For the ascendancy, check the current guide, which includes Way of the Stonefist.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early EndgameYou are here
  1. Find out why your charges are dropping
  2. Swap supports depending on whether you have mana problems
  3. Stabilize Evasion, ES and resistances first
Main skill
Whirling Assault as the clearing main skill, Falling Thunder as charge spender and single-target damage.
Supports
For Whirling Assault, swap between Efficiency II and critical supports depending on your mana situation. Add reflection / area supports to Falling Thunder in stages.
Passives
Complete the Early Endgame tree before moving to Late Endgame. Avoid changing all passives at once.
Gear priorities
Melee skill levels, critical strike chance, Evasion, maximum ES, global defenses, and any missing resistances.
Replacement candidates
If defenses are unstable, prioritize Evasion, ES and resistances over damage gear.
Caution
There are other charge methods such as Hollow Focus, but the current guide treats the Charged Staff approach as the stable option.
When to move to the next stage
Charge generation and mana sustain are stable, and your upgrade goals toward Late Endgame gear are clear.

BUILD PROFILE

Whirling Assault Martial Artist: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Whirling Assault lets you attack while moving, so maps flow smoothly.
  • Combined with Falling Thunder, you can convert charges into single-target damage or wide-area attacks.
  • A leveling route from Lv1 to Lv41 is provided.
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) lists "SSF Campaign ~4 hours, Arbiter ~8 hours" among its strengths.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Whirling Assault cannot be used until Lv41, so you have to switch skills partway through the campaign.
  • The early Glacial Cascade route is described as having low durability, so keeping gear updated and dodging matter.
  • In Endgame you need to generate the first Power Charge and keep Charged Staff up, so it is not a complete one-button setup.
  • The main skill, Whirling Assault, is a Tier 11 gem usable only from Lv41 (poe2db). Until then you progress with other skills.

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon: added damage, increased damage, +levels to melee / attack skills, critical, attack speed
  2. Boots: movement speed → resistances, Life, Evasion
  3. Armor: resistances, Life, Evasion. In Endgame, also value ES and global defenses
