Monk / Acolyte of Chayula

Main skill: A minion army setup whose main force is Spectres bound with Bind Spectre. Late in the campaign it is bomb-type Vaal Guards; from mapping, Powered Zealot / Chaos Zealot Spectres firing Spark carry the damage, and Waking Dream's purple flames are transferred to the minions with Selfless Remnants to stack added chaos damage

LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Lightning Minion Acolyte of Chayula leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–End of Act 3 (crossbow / grenade leveling)You are here
  1. Pick up a crossbow and switch to grenade leveling
  2. Secure movement speed and resistances on your boots
  3. Secure gear with +minion skills when you see it
Main skill
For the first few levels, progress with Quarterstaff Strike, Falling Thunder and Killing Palm. Once you pick up a Makeshift Crossbow or buy one from Renly in Clearfell, Explosive Grenade becomes the main skill. Flash Grenade covers both pack clearing and boss stuns, and while both are on cooldown you fill in with Crossbow Shot
Supports
Multishot I and Short Fuse I on Explosive Grenade. Putting Lightning Attunement on Flash Grenade grows its damage and also applies Shock. Use the first Lesser Jeweller's Orb on Explosive Grenade, the second on Flash Grenade, and save the rest for the minion setup (source).
Passives
Grow grenade damage with Concussive Attack, Dizzying Sweep, Killer Instinct, Blinding Strike and Kite Runner (source).
Gear priorities
Prioritize a crossbow with high flat physical and lightning damage, and switch to a Bombard Crossbow early in Act 3. Armor: maximum Life and resistances; Armour or Armour / Evasion bases are easier to progress on. If you see a helmet, amulet or sceptre with +1 minion skills for after the transition, secure it (source).
Replacement candidates
Update flasks to the newest ones every 10–15 levels (source). Killing The King in the Mists makes War Banner usable; after defeating the witch in The Azak Bog, switch to Wolf Pack. Apply Exposure with Frost Bomb and curse with Elemental Weakness (source).
Caution
This stage is not a minion setup (source weakness: Non-Minion leveling in Act 1-3). The source says Evasion-only armor makes the early game feel rough. Pick up rares, identify them, then sell them to secure gold for the transition.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Utzaal late in Act 3 and become able to bind a Vaal Guard Spectre (around Lv41–43).
Source stage: Grow grenade damage with Concussive Attack, Dizzying Sweep, Killer Instinct, Blinding Strike and Kite Runner (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Around Lv41–43, Late Act 3–Campaign End (minion transition)You are here
  1. Bind a Vaal Guard (bomb type)
  2. Respec your passives for about 35,000 gold
  3. Maintain Raging Spirits with Flame Wall
Main skill
Bind the bomb-type Vaal Guard with Bind Spectre and transition to the minion setup. Spam Flame Wall to bring out Raging Spirits, and protect them with Wolf Pack and Loyalty. Spend leftover Spirit on a Skeletal Storm Mage or Skeletal Sniper to supplement the Muster effect and Shock application (source)
Supports
Cut Loyalty, Minion Splash I, Last Gasp, Arcane Surge and Selfless Remnants with leftover Uncut Support Gems. Use Flame Wall at a low level (Lv5) to keep its Mana cost down. If you obtain a Greater Jeweller's Orb, there is also the option of replacing the Vaal Guard with a Skeletal Reaver (source).
Passives
Respec into minion specialization for about 35,000 gold. The core is Relentless Fallen, Lord of Horrors, Remnant Attraction and Empowering Remnants, Right Hand of Darkness, Necrotised Flesh and Crystalline Flesh, Vile Mending, and Profane Commander. The ascendancy order is Waking Dream → Choice of Power (source).
Gear priorities
+levels to all minion skills is the top priority (sceptre, helmet, amulet). The sceptre mod "allies in your Presence gain added damage to attacks" scales multiplicatively with Choice of Power and Waking Dream, so its growth is large. Increase summon counts with additional Spirit on the chest and amulet (source).
Replacement candidates
If you obtain Enfolding Dawn and Bones of Ullr, use them. The Lv36 Soul Mantle is also a defensive option, but runeforging Enfolding Dawn is considered best. A Trenchtimbre with +2 minion skills is also a candidate as hand-me-down leveling gear (source).
Caution
There are three different variants of Vaal Guard under the same name (melee type = 50 Spirit, ranged type = 60 Spirit, bomb type = 50 Spirit). Bind the type that throws bombs. The source also warns this Spectre can cause performance problems. Right after the respec there is a stretch where damage is lower than grenade leveling (source).
When to move to the next stage
Finish the campaign, enter mapping, and become able to switch to Powered Zealot / Chaos Zealot Spectres.
Source stage: Respec into minion specialization for about 35,000 gold. The core is Relentless Fallen, Lord of Horrors, Remnant Attraction and Empowering Remnants, Right Hand of Darkness, Necrotised Flesh and Crystalline Flesh, Vile Mending, and Profane Commander. The ascendancy order is Waking Dream → Choice of Power (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early Mapping (Spark Spectres)You are here
  1. Bind a Powered Zealot (55 Spirit, Spark type)
  2. Obtain Alpha's Howl
  3. Secure about 100 Ward and run Wardbound Minions
Main skill
Replace the Vaal Guard with a Powered Zealot or Chaos Zealot Spectre that fires Spark. Powered Zealots are found in maps, and Chaos Zealots can be found in the Trial of Chaos. You support the army with Pain Offering and Despair (source)
Supports
As a prerequisite for the switch, cut Wildshards II and Pierce III first. Use the first Perfect Jeweller's Orb on Bind Spectre. Replace the Skeletal Storm Mage with Wardbound Minions and combine it with Warding Rune of Bodyguards to grant minions Onslaught. Also secure Refutation (source).
Passives
Grow ES and Evasion with Enhanced Barrier, Mindful Awareness and Spectral Ward, and reduce minions' Spirit reservation with The Soul Meridian. The amulet instill is Essence Infusion (source).
Gear priorities
Obtain Alpha's Howl. Spirit grows greatly, and the Presence radius when using Selfless Remnants + Waking Dream doubles. If it is expensive, substitute a helmet with +2 minion skills. For Bones of Ullr, prioritize reservation efficiency and movement speed. Runeforge Enfolding Dawn (source).
Replacement candidates
A Rattling Sceptre is the best sceptre, but with a Shrine Sceptre you can use Upwelling II, Strong Hearted, Cool Headed and Vitality II at no additional Spirit cost. Maximize ES on gloves. For resistance adjustments, use Blazing Flux, Chilling Flux, Crackling Flux and Void Flux (source).
Caution
Powered Zealots / Chaos Zealots also have different variants under the same name. They cost 55 Spirit; let them attack a few times to confirm they fire the big blue lightning orbs (Spark) before binding. Maintaining Wardbound Minions requires about 100 Ward, and the gem level should be at most 16, preferably low (source).
When to move to the next stage
Graduate from Flame Wall and Raging Spirits, and transition to the Mid setup (Skeletal Storm Mage + Convalescence).
Source stage: Grow ES and Evasion with Enhanced Barrier, Mindful Awareness and Spectral Ward, and reduce minions' Spirit reservation with The Soul Meridian. The amulet instill is Essence Infusion (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Mid (army complete)You are here
  1. Turn the curse into an aura with Blasphemy + Despair
  2. Obtain a Prism Guardian
  3. Solidify defense with Heavy Buffer + Mind Over Matter
Main skill
Replace Flame Wall and Raging Spirits with Skeletal Storm Mage and Convalescence. Load Despair onto Blasphemy to turn it into a curse aura, and strengthen the army with Pain Offering. This is the polished stretch where the source's strengths include "Tanky (In the Mid setup)" and "Few buttons in the Mid setup" (source)
Supports
Swap Pain Offering's support from Meat Shield II to Brutus' Brain. Put Romira's Requital and Tecrod's Revenge on Wolf Pack (source).
Passives
Use Heavy Buffer and Mind Over Matter to use Mana as a defensive layer. Inner Faith and The Hollowkeeper reduce curse effect on you by 75%. Swap the campaign flask rewards to Mana flask upgrades. The amulet instill is Warlord Leader (source).
Gear priorities
Extend Spirit with a Prism Guardian. Update Alpha's Howl to a corrupted version with "+1 to all minion skill levels". Once Spirit exceeds 450, insert Amanamu's Gaze into Bones of Ullr to grow movement speed. Also prepare a Lavianga's Spirits Mana flask (source).
Replacement candidates
Choose chest and gloves bases with high ES and Evasion; having Deflect on both is strongly recommended. Put Warding Rune of Bodyguards in the sceptre, a Primate Idol in the second socket, and a Talisman of Ralakesh in the helmet (source).
Caution
From this stage it becomes tanky, but as the source's weaknesses state, it is not recommended for HC. Maintaining Wardbound Minions requires securing about 100 Ward through runeforging Bones of Ullr and the gloves (or a Prism Guardian if that is not enough) (source).
When to move to the next stage
Secure +55% or more minion cast speed and advance to the Late setup (the switch to Vilenta's Propulsion).
Source stage: Use Heavy Buffer and Mind Over Matter to use Mana as a defensive layer. Inner Faith and The Hollowkeeper reduce curse effect on you by 75%. Swap the campaign flask rewards to Mana flask upgrades. The amulet instill is Warlord Leader (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
LateYou are here
  1. Secure the rare belt's additional minion projectile mod
  2. Obtain a high-ES focus with +2 minion skill levels
  3. Confirm +55% cast speed, then switch to Vilenta's Propulsion
Main skill
Once minion cast speed reaches +55% or more, replace Projectile Acceleration II with Vilenta's Propulsion. Put Uhtred's Constellation on Convalescence (source)
Supports
Removing Blasphemy and self-casting Despair grows damage for pinnacle bosses and lets you add 2–3 more Spectres (source FAQ). In that case, put Heightened Curse and similar on Despair.
Passives
Keep the amulet instill at Warlord Leader. Add minion critical stats and faster ES recharge start to Sapphire jewels (source).
Gear priorities
The most important is the rare belt mod "chance for minions to fire an additional projectile (Surpassing)". Use a high-ES focus with +2 minion skill levels. Put Cadigan's Epiphany in the gloves. Alpha's Howl can also aim for a double-corrupted version (+20–30 Spirit, or an additional socket) (source).
Replacement candidates
Replace both rings with ones carrying minion mods (minion damage, attack / cast speed, minion resistances) (source).
Caution
If you switch to Vilenta's Propulsion before meeting the +55% cast speed condition, damage drops. Check the condition first (source).
When to move to the next stage
The amulet instill changes to Zarokh's Gift, and the Min-Max setup gear such as Sacrificial Regalia comes together.
Source stage: Keep the amulet instill at Warlord Leader. Add minion critical stats and faster ES recharge start to Sapphire jewels (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Min-Max (finished form)You are here
  1. Change the instill to Zarokh's Gift
  2. Aim for an Alpha's Howl with Legacy of Greymake
  3. Replace Wolf Pack with Ghost Dance or a Skeletal Sniper
Main skill
Replace Wolf Pack with Ghost Dance or a Skeletal Sniper to optimize Spirit. Put Khatal's Rejuvenation on Into the Breach (can be omitted if expensive). Using the Remnant snapshot (entering with Weapon Set II) grows damage a further 20–30%, but it is an advanced optimization the source calls completely optional (source)
Supports
Replace the Powered Zealot / Chaos Zealot's Feeding Frenzy II with Dialla's Desire, and the Skeletal Storm Mage's Lightning Attunement with Esh's Radiance. Put Atziri's Communion on Blasphemy or Convalescence (source).
Passives
The amulet instill is Zarokh's Gift. If you obtain a Megalomaniac with Right Hand of Darkness, respec the pathing toward it (source).
Gear priorities
If Sacrificial Regalia comes together with the right mods, it is the best chest. The finished Alpha's Howl is 2 sockets + Legacy of Greymake, either corrupted with "+1 to all minion skill levels" or double-corrupted with +25–30 Spirit. A Mageblood is also an option instead of the rare belt (source).
Replacement candidates
Replace the Amanamu's Gaze in Bones of Ullr with Farrul's Rune of the Chase, and secure movement speed with the Quicksilver-type effect of Legacy of Greymake (source).
Caution
The source warns that if you do not snapshot with the snapshot tree, you lose about 50% of your total damage. If you will not do it, stay on the regular tree. The fourth ascendancy is stated in the source to be "completely useless", so there is no need to hurry it (source).
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, rerolling jewels and corruptions is the focus.
Source stage: The amulet instill is Zarokh's Gift. If you obtain a Megalomaniac with Right Hand of Darkness, respec the pathing toward it (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source

BUILD PROFILE

Lightning Minion Acolyte of Chayula: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Very high damage (source strength: Very high damage). On top of the Spark-firing Spectre army, the added chaos damage from Waking Dream and Choice of Power rides along.
  • Tanky from the Mid setup onward (source strength: Tanky (In the Mid setup)). Stacking ES and Evasion, plus damage reduction from Loyalty and Wolf Pack and Refutation's block.
  • Few buttons from the Mid setup onward (source strength: Few buttons in the Mid setup). The minions do the processing, and you mainly maintain Pain Offering and Despair.
  • No unique is mandatory for the campaign to function (source). It grows with common uniques such as Enfolding Dawn and Bones of Ullr.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Acts 1–3 are not minion leveling (source weakness: Non-Minion leveling in Act 1-3). You progress with a crossbow and grenades, and a respec of about 35,000 gold is needed late in Act 3.
  • Can be squishy until the Mid setup (source weakness: Can be squishy until Mid setup).
  • Not recommended for HC (source weakness: Not recommended for HC).
  • You must watch for Spectre variants. Both Vaal Guards and Powered Zealots / Chaos Zealots have different variants under the same name, and you must bind the right type (for Vaal Guards the bomb type; for the Zealots the 55-Spirit type that fires big blue lightning orbs) (source).
  • Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source.

Gear priorities

  1. +levels to all minion skills: secure it on the sceptre, helmet and amulet. The top-priority stat, growing both minion damage and durability (source).
  2. Spirit: decides how many Spectres you can have. Alpha's Howl grows it greatly, and also doubles the Presence radius when using Selfless Remnants (source).
  3. Bones of Ullr: obtain it prioritizing reservation efficiency to increase the number of minions you can summon (source).
  4. Defense: stack ES and Evasion on the chest and gloves. Having Deflect on both is strongly recommended (source).
  5. Enfolding Dawn: runeforging it is considered the best defensive option (source).
  6. Jewels: on Sapphires, prioritize increased maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, and increased minion damage (source).

FAQ

Lightning Minion Acolyte of Chayula build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who like letting a minion army do the fighting while they focus on debuffs and support. From the Mid setup onward it runs on few buttons

What weakness should I know before starting?

Acts 1–3 are not minion leveling (source weakness: Non-Minion leveling in Act 1-3). You progress with a crossbow and grenades, and a respec of about 35,000 gold is needed late in Act 3.
