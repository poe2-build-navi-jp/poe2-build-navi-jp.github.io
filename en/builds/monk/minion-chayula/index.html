Into the Breach's Remnants are narrowed to added chaos damage by Choice of Power and transferred to the minions with Selfless Remnants to grow damage. LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Minion Army Acolyte of Chayula leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–37 (crossbow / grenade leveling)You are here
  1. Obtain a crossbow
  2. Proceed with Explosive Grenade and Flash Grenade
  3. Prioritize maximum Life and resistances on gear
Main skill
Progress the first 2–3 levels with a Quarterstaff, and once you have a Makeshift Crossbow (can be bought from Renly), proceed with Explosive Grenade and Flash Grenade. Apply Exposure with Frost Bomb and raise boss damage with War Banner
Supports
Explosive Grenade: Multishot II and Elemental Armament I. Flash Grenade: Multishot II and Lightning Attunement. Frost Bomb: Rapid Casting I. Into the Breach unlocks after taking the first ascendancy (Remnant Potency II + Harmonic Remnants II).
Passives
Raise grenade damage with Concussive Attack, Dizzying Sweep, Killer Instinct, Blinding Strike and Kite Runner. The first ascendancy order is Waking Dream → Choice of Power (source).
Gear priorities
A crossbow with high physical / lightning damage (a Bombard Crossbow in Act 3). Prioritize maximum Life and resistances. Armour or Armour / Evasion bases are preferable, but the source says Evasion-only is rough early on. If you see a helmet, amulet or sceptre with +1 minion skills, secure it (for the switch late in Act 3).
Replacement candidates
Update flasks to the newest ones every 10–15 levels (source).
Caution
The source calls the early part of Monk's Act 1 a rough section. Do not overextend until Explosive Grenade unlocks. Pick up and sell rares, and start saving gold for the respec.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Utzaal late in Act 3 and become able to bind a Vaal Guard Spectre (around Lv38).
Source stage: Raise grenade damage with Concussive Attack, Dizzying Sweep, Killer Instinct, Blinding Strike and Kite Runner. The first ascendancy order is Waking Dream → Choice of Power (source).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv38–41 (switch to minions: Vaal Guard Spectre)You are here
  1. Bind a bomb-throwing Vaal Guard
  2. Bring out Raging Spirits with Flame Wall
  3. Respec your passives for about 35,000 gold
Main skill
Bind a bomb-throwing Vaal Guard with Bind Spectre, and bring out Raging Spirits with Flame Wall. Strengthen the minions with Wolf Pack + Pain Offering + Despair
Supports
Vaal Guard Spectre: Multishot II + Feeding Frenzy I + Muster. Raging Spirits: Last Gasp + Minion Splash I + Feeding Frenzy. Pain Offering: Meat Shield II + Danse Macabre. Into the Breach: Remnant Potency II + Harmonic Remnants II + Selfless Remnants.
Passives
Respec for about 35,000 gold around Lv41–43. Relentless Fallen for minion mobility and damage; Lord of Horrors and The Soul Meridian to reduce Spirit reservation; Remnant Attraction and Empowering Remnants to strengthen Waking Dream and Choice of Power.
Gear priorities
A sceptre, helmet and amulet with +levels to all minion skills. A Rattling Sceptre is the best base, but mod content matters more than the base (source). A high roll of added damage to allies in your Presence (fire / cold / lightning / physical) grows damage greatly, because Choice of Power and Waking Dream multiply it.
Replacement candidates
Replace helmet, amulet and sceptre with +1–2 minion skill versions. If you have leftover Spirit, add one Skeletal Storm Mage.
Caution
There are three monsters named Vaal Guard (melee / 50 Spirit, ranged / 60 Spirit, bomb type / 50 Spirit). Bind the type that throws bombs. Bomb-type Spectres can cause performance problems (source note). If your PC cannot handle it or you find it unpleasant, you can substitute a Skeletal Reaver. Right after the switch, damage is lower than grenade leveling (stated in the source).
When to move to the next stage
The Vaal Guard Spectre is bound, the passive respec (about 35,000 gold) is done, and you can enter Act 4 on the minion setup.
Source stage: Respec for about 35,000 gold around Lv41–43. Relentless Fallen for minion mobility and damage; Lord of Horrors and The Soul Meridian to reduce Spirit reservation; Remnant Attraction and Empowering Remnants to strengthen Waking Dream and Choice of Power.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv42–59 (Skeletal Reaver: Act 4–Interludes)You are here
  1. Use the Greater Jeweller's Orb on a Skeletal Reaver
  2. Use the defensive skills found in Expedition
  3. Supplement healing with a Skeletal Cleric
Main skill
Use the first Greater Jeweller's Orb on a Skeletal Reaver and replace the Vaal Guard. Apply Shock with a Skeletal Storm Mage and heal with a Skeletal Cleric. Put Onslaught on minions with Refutation, and block with it in dangerous moments
Supports
Skeletal Reaver: Muster + Feeding Frenzy II + Last Gasp + Rage III. Raging Spirits: Muster + Minion Splash II + Feeding Frenzy II + Last Gasp. Pain Offering: Meat Shield II + Danse Macabre + Elemental Army. Refutation: Prolonged Duration II + Cooldown Recovery II.
Passives
Take Right Hand of Darkness, because Withered scales greatly with Waking Dream and Choice of Power. Raise minion survivability with Necrotised Flesh, Crystalline Flesh and Vile Mending. Widen Spirit and Presence area with Profane Commander.
Gear priorities
Maintain +levels to all minion skills on the sceptre, helmet and amulet. With a Shrine Sceptre, you can use Spirit-reservation supports such as Upwelling II effectively for free (source). Prioritize boots with movement speed, Life and resistances.
Replacement candidates
At Lv36, Soul Mantle becomes a defensive option. If you have Enfolding Dawn, runeforging it is best (source).
Caution
Refutation and Wardbound Minions are skill gems from Expedition encounters. If you find them, keep them. Wardbound Minions is not used until the Mid setup.
When to move to the next stage
Finish the Interludes and enter maps. The Skeletal Reaver setup is in place and you can use Refutation.
Source stage: Take Right Hand of Darkness, because Withered scales greatly with Waking Dream and Choice of Power. Raise minion survivability with Necrotised Flesh, Crystalline Flesh and Vile Mending. Widen Spirit and Presence area with Profane Commander.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv60–79 (Early Mapping)You are here
  1. Obtain the helmet that doubles Presence radius
  2. Cap resistances and meet attribute requirements
  3. Runeforge a high-ES chest piece
Main skill
Keep the Skeletal Reaver as the main force and keep bringing out Raging Spirits with Flame Wall. Strengthen all minions with Pain Offering, and weaken bosses and tough rares with Despair. Use Refutation on cooldown to put Onslaught on minions
Supports
Skeletal Reaver: Muster + Feeding Frenzy II + Last Gasp + Rage III + Minion Splash II. Pain Offering: Meat Shield II + Danse Macabre + Magnified Area II + Prolonged Duration II. Despair: Heightened Curse + Cursed Ground + Efficiency II + Prolonged Duration II. Refutation: Cooldown Recovery II + Prolonged Duration II + Rapid Casting II + Mobility.
Passives
Raise ES and Evasion with Enhanced Barrier, Mindful Awareness and Spectral Ward. Greatly extend ES with Heavy Buffer, and combine it with Mind Over Matter to use Mana for defense. Inner Faith and The Hollowkeeper reduce curse effect on you by 75%. The third ascendancy is Chayula's Gift.
Gear priorities
Alpha's Howl is the top priority (large Spirit increase + doubled Presence radius). Runeforge Enfolding Dawn, and increase minion counts with Bones of Ullr. Gloves prioritize ES above all. Instill Essence Infusion on the amulet.
Replacement candidates
If Alpha's Howl is expensive, substitute a helmet with +2 minion skills (source). The sceptre is a Shrine Sceptre or a Rattling Sceptre. Boots (Bones of Ullr) gain Ward through runeforging.
Caution
First cap your resistances and meet attribute requirements. Until elemental resistances are capped, adjust with Flux runes. Without about 100 Ward you cannot maintain Wardbound Minions, so transition to the Mid setup only after securing Ward.
When to move to the next stage
Obtain a rare belt with "minion attacks have melee splash", and Spirit reaches 450 or more.
Source stage: Raise ES and Evasion with Enhanced Barrier, Mindful Awareness and Spectral Ward. Greatly extend ES with Heavy Buffer, and combine it with Mind Over Matter to use Mana for defense. Inner Faith and The Hollowkeeper reduce curse effect on you by 75%. The third ascendancy is Chayula's Gift.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv80–89 (Mid: Plague Harvester)You are here
  1. Obtain a rare belt with melee splash
  2. Switch to a Plague Harvester Spectre
  3. Secure Ward up to about 100
Main skill
Switch to a Plague Harvester Spectre. It moves fast and leaps over obstacles, so it does not get stuck like the Skeletal Reaver. Remove Flame Wall and Raging Spirits, and add Wardbound Minions (max Lv16) and Convalescence. Turn Despair into an aura with Blasphemy
Supports
Plague Harvester Spectre: Muster + Feeding Frenzy II + Rage III + Rapid Attacks II + Minion Mastery. Pain Offering: Danse Macabre + Magnified Area II + Prolonged Duration II + Brutus' Brain + Efficiency II. Blasphemy + Despair: Ritualistic Curse + Magnified Area II + Chaos Mastery. Wardbound Minions: Efficiency II + Blind II + Rapid Casting II + Mobility. Convalescence: Second Wind III + Cooldown Recovery II + Prolonged Duration II + Rapid Casting III.
Passives
Use Heavy Buffer and Mind Over Matter to use Mana for defense. Change the amulet instill to Warlord Leader. Sapphire jewels prioritize maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, minion damage, and faster ES recharge start. Optional: specialize Weapon Set II in Remnant nodes and snapshot by switching Set II → Set I on zone entry for about 20–30% more damage (if you will not do it, proceed on the base tree).
Gear priorities
A rare belt with "minion attacks have melee splash" is required. Maximize ES and Evasion on the chest and gloves, and put Deflection Rating on at least one piece, preferably both. The chest is a Rambler Jacket or a Sleek Jacket. Add Spirit with a Prism Guardian. At 450+ Spirit, insert Amanamu's Gaze into the boots to raise movement speed.
Replacement candidates
Update Alpha's Howl to a corrupted version with "+1 minion skills". Bones of Ullr may prioritize reservation efficiency over movement speed (if Spirit is 450+). Secure about 100 Ward on the gloves through runeforging.
Caution
Wardbound Minions is at most Lv16; lower is better because it consumes less Ward. Without about 100 Ward you cannot maintain Onslaught. Without the melee splash belt, the Spectre loses one support slot.
When to move to the next stage
ES and Evasion have grown enough, and you are used to running Convalescence and Refutation. You can put Khatal's Rejuvenation into Into the Breach.
Source stage: Use Heavy Buffer and Mind Over Matter to use Mana for defense. Change the amulet instill to Warlord Leader. Sapphire jewels prioritize maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, minion damage, and faster ES recharge start. Optional: specialize Weapon Set II in Remnant nodes and snapshot by switching Set II → Set I on zone entry for about 20–30% more damage (if you will not do it, proceed on the base tree).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv90+ (Late–Min-Max finished form)You are here
  1. Change the amulet instill to the late-game one
  2. Set up a system that can grant minions Rage
  3. Update the Spectre's supports to their final form
Main skill
Recover ES with Convalescence, block enemy attacks with Refutation, and strengthen all minions with Pain Offering. In Min-Max, take Bulwark with From Nothing, enable Warlord Berserker with a Megalomaniac carrying Blood of Rage, and grant minions Rage with the Blood of the Warrior flask
Supports
Plague Harvester Spectre: Feeding Frenzy II + Muster + Dialla's Desire + Uul-Netol's Embrace + Minion Splash II (per the text's instructions; the planner has Rapid Attacks II, and the discrepancy is noted). Pain Offering: Danse Macabre + Magnified Area II + Prolonged Duration II + Brutus' Brain + Sacrificial Offering. Convalescence: Uhtred's Constellation. Skeletal Storm Mage: Esh's Radiance.
Passives
Take Bulwark with From Nothing, and take Warlord Leader, Warlord Berserker and Ally Attack Damage. The Megalomaniac carries Blood of Rage. Sapphire jewels prioritize maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, minion damage, minion critical strike chance and critical damage bonus, and faster ES recharge start.
Gear priorities
Maximize ES and Evasion on the chest and gloves, and add Deflect. The chest is a Rambler Jacket or a Sleek Jacket. Insert Legacy of Greymake into a 2-socket Alpha's Howl (corrupted with +1 minion skills). A high-ES focus (+2 minion skills). If you replace the belt with a Mageblood, return Minion Splash II to the Spectre.
Replacement candidates
Change the amulet instill to Zarokh's Gift. Insert Cadigan's Epiphany into the gloves. If you have Sacrificial Regalia, it is a finished-form chest candidate. In addition to Lavianga's Spirits, prepare a Blood of the Warrior flask.
Caution
If you use the Blood of the Warrior flask while Rage is at maximum, Warlord Berserker does not trigger. Use it after Rage has dropped. From 0 Rage, using it in three separate doses maxes the minions' Rage (source). The source states the fourth ascendancy is useless.
Completion condition
After finishing, progress by rerolling the Spectre and updating jewels. For pinnacle bosses, removing Blasphemy and self-casting Despair raises damage (source FAQ).
Source stage: Take Bulwark with From Nothing, and take Warlord Leader, Warlord Berserker and Ally Attack Damage. The Megalomaniac carries Blood of Rage. Sapphire jewels prioritize maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, minion damage, minion critical strike chance and critical damage bonus, and faster ES recharge start.View this stage's passive tree in the source

BUILD PROFILE

Minion Army Acolyte of Chayula: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Very high damage (source strength: Very high damage). Added damage to allies in your Presence grows multiplicatively through Choice of Power and Waking Dream.
  • Tanky defense in the Mid setup (source strength: Tanky (In the Mid setup)). On top of ES + Evasion, damage reduction from Loyalty and Wolf Pack and Refutation's block stack up.
  • Few buttons in the Mid setup (source strength: Few buttons in the Mid setup). Maintaining Pain Offering and Wardbound Minions is the core, and you can focus on curses and debuffs yourself.
  • No unique is mandatory during the campaign. It can be grown with cheap options such as Enfolding Dawn and Bones of Ullr (source).
  • The Plague Harvester moves fast and leaps over obstacles, so it rarely gets stuck or caught like the Skeletal Reaver (source).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Not recommended for HC (source weakness: Not recommended for HC).
  • Acts 1–3 leveling is not minions (source weakness: Non-Minion leveling in Act 1-3). A respec of about 35,000 gold is needed around Lv41–43, and right after the switch damage is lower than grenade leveling (stated in the source).
  • Can be squishy until the Mid setup (source weakness: Can be squishy until Mid setup).
  • Vaal Guard Spectres can cause performance problems (source note). If your PC cannot handle it or you find it unpleasant, you can substitute a Skeletal Reaver.
  • The Remnant snapshot (optional) requires weapon set management, and if you take it but do not execute it, damage drops by about 50% (source). You can also ignore it and proceed on the base tree.
  • Whether the build works in SSF is not stated in the source.

Gear priorities

  1. +levels to all minion skills: secure it on the sceptre, helmet and amulet. The top-priority stat, growing both minion damage and durability (source).
  2. Spirit: increase the number of minions you can summon with additional Spirit from gear. Alpha's Howl is the top priority for its large Spirit increase + doubled Presence radius. A Prism Guardian is also used to add Spirit (source).
  3. Added damage to allies in your Presence: a high roll of this sceptre mod grows damage greatly, because Choice of Power and Waking Dream multiply it (source).
  4. A melee splash belt: a requirement for the Mid setup. A rare belt with "minion attacks have melee splash" frees one support slot on the Spectre (source).
  5. Defense: stack ES and Evasion, and reduce damage taken with Loyalty and Wolf Pack. In the Mid setup, ES + Evasion + about 100 Ward is the foundation (source).
  6. Jewels: on Sapphire jewels, prioritize maximum ES, minions' all elemental resistances, minion attack / cast speed, and minion damage. From Mid onward, faster ES recharge start; in Min-Max, minion critical as well (source).
