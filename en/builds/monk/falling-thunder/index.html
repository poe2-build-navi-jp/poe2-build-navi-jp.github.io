LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Falling Thunder Martial Artist leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–15 (Act 1, Quarterstaff)You are here
  1. Place Frozen Locus and break it with Quarterstaff Strike or Wind Blast
  2. On bosses, make 3 Power Charges with Wing Blast before firing Falling Thunder
  3. Upgrade to a Gothic Quarterstaff at Lv11
Main skill
For packs, Frozen Locus + Herald of Ice. For single target, Falling Thunder
Supports
Falling Thunder: Elemental Armament I → Lightning Attunement. Frozen Locus: Elemental Armament I → Cold Attunement. Wind Blast: Rage I → Rapid Attacks I. Herald of Ice: Magnified Area I. The support setup is covered only as far as it was cross-checked through a secondary summary of the source planner's staged table; for the latest exact slots, see the source guide.
Passives
Follow the source's staged "Campaign" tree. There is a first respec at Lv10, so keep some gold in reserve.
Gear priorities
Use the Orb of Transmutation obtained from Clearfell's league mechanic reward on a Wrapped Quarterstaff. Look for a Long Quarterstaff in Red Vale. Armor: Life / ES. Gloves and rings: added damage to attacks. Boots: movement speed.
Replacement candidates
At Lv11, check Renly's Gothic Quarterstaff, craft it with your currency and upgrade. Open sockets and insert elemental runes.
Caution
Act 1 is called a difficult section even in the source. In boss fights, damage is reduced for the first 5 seconds after the fight starts, so wait a little before spending Power Charges. Boss sequence: prepare a stun with Wind Blast → stun with Wing Blast for 3 Power Charges → spend 1 with Rend for the lightning buff → place Frost Bomb at their feet → Falling Thunder with the remaining 2.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv16 and have the gold for the second respec saved. Transition to Hollow Palm Technique.
Source stage: Follow the source's staged "Campaign" tree. There is a first respec at Lv10, so keep some gold in reserve.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv16–End of Act 2 (Hollow Palm transition)You are here
  1. Respec into Hollow Palm Technique at Lv16
  2. Secure +levels to melee skills on gloves and amulet
  3. Finish the Trial of the Sekhemas at Lv22 and take Hollow Focus Technique
Main skill
Unarmed attacks with Hollow Palm Technique are the core. Single target is Falling Thunder
Supports
During Hollow Palm you use no weapon and scale damage with melee skill levels. The Act 2 single-target loop: Shock with Storm Wave → consume the Shock with Siphoning Strike for a Power Charge → Falling Thunder. For exact support slots, see the source planner.
Passives
Respec into Hollow Palm Technique at Lv16. At Lv22, finish the Trial of the Sekhemas and take Hollow Focus Technique as your first ascendancy.
Gear priorities
Remove the Quarterstaff. +levels to melee skills on gloves and amulet is the top priority. Armor: Life / Evasion / ES and resistances.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize gloves / amulets with +levels to melee skills. You do not need to upgrade a weapon in this period, so save crafting currency.
Caution
Devour a Hollow Focus bell before a boss and you can start the fight with 3 Power Charges. Also keep Frost Bomb's Exposure up.
When to move to the next stage
Push through Acts 3–4 still on Hollow Palm.
Source stage: Respec into Hollow Palm Technique at Lv16. At Lv22, finish the Trial of the Sekhemas and take Hollow Focus Technique as your first ascendancy.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Act 3–Campaign End (Hollow Palm continues)You are here
  1. Push through the campaign still on Hollow Palm
  2. Save your crafting currency
  3. Before bosses, store Power Charges by Devouring a bell
Main skill
Hollow Palm unarmed attacks + Falling Thunder
Supports
Continue the campaign setup. Maintain the Thrill of the Kill buff with Devour / Killing Palm finishers, and do not let Rend's lightning buff drop in boss fights. For exact support slots, see the source planner.
Passives
Continue the source's "Campaign" tree. The second ascendancy is Way of the Stonefist: glove mods are converted and scale — attack speed → chance for Onslaught, added damage → gained as added damage, defenses → Evasion / ES.
Gear priorities
Keep updating gloves / amulet with +levels to melee skills. Start leaning armor toward Evasion / ES and bring resistances close to the cap.
Replacement candidates
Update gloves with added damage and +levels to melee skills as the top priority.
Caution
Hollow Palm starts to fall off late. You do not need weapon upgrades in this period, so save your crafting currency instead of spending it.
When to move to the next stage
Reach the Interludes and Lv54, with the crafting materials for a Waxing Quarterstaff ready.
Source stage: Continue the source's "Campaign" tree. The second ascendancy is Way of the Stonefist: glove mods are converted and scale — attack speed → chance for Onslaught, added damage → gained as added damage, defenses → Evasion / ES.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Interludes (Lv54, return to Quarterstaff)You are here
  1. Craft a Waxing Quarterstaff at Lv54
  2. Remove Hollow Palm Technique
  3. Start securing gems for the Whirling Assault setup
Main skill
Waxing Quarterstaff + Falling Thunder
Supports
You are returning to a weapon, so return Falling Thunder's supports to the weapon-based setup. For exact slots, see the source planner.
Passives
Remove Hollow Palm Technique and rebuild the tree around a Quarterstaff.
Gear priorities
Crafting a Waxing Quarterstaff with your saved currency is the top priority.
Replacement candidates
Replace gear that was all-in on +levels to melee skills for the unarmed setup with regular added damage / critical gear, piece by piece.
Caution
If you leave Hollow Palm's falloff unaddressed, you will stall in the Interludes. Once you hit Lv54, return to a weapon promptly.
When to move to the next stage
Finish the campaign. Start stockpiling gems for the Whirling Assault setup for maps.
Source stage: Remove Hollow Palm Technique and rebuild the tree around a Quarterstaff.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Start of Mapping (respec to Whirling Assault)You are here
  1. Respec into the Whirling Assault setup
  2. Make Ailith's Chimes your first purchase goal
  3. Do not let the Charged Staff buff drop
Main skill
Build energy with Whirling Assault, generate Power Charges with Reaper's Invocation → Profane Ritual. Spend them on Charged Staff and Falling Thunder
Supports
Whirling Assault is for building energy; Falling Thunder carries the damage. Inserting Mantra of Destruction adds chaos damage in proportion to the combo consumed.
Passives
Respec into the mapping tree. Switch to the routine of processing a Hollow Focus bell with Killing Palm before bosses to store Power Charges.
Gear priorities
The first purchase candidate is Ailith's Chimes. Whirling Assault has no inherent conversion, so the source advises that an elemental damage Quarterstaff is advantageous.
Replacement candidates
Secure Ailith's Chimes as the top priority. After that, grow the weapon's elemental damage and critical.
Caution
Until you have Ailith's Chimes, the charge engine is not in its finished form. The opening charge generation sometimes uses Tempest Bell.
When to move to the next stage
Obtain Ailith's Chimes and take Martial Master.
Source stage: Respec into the mapping tree. Switch to the routine of processing a Hollow Focus bell with Killing Palm before bosses to store Power Charges.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (Finished) (CI, charge engine complete)You are here
  1. Maintain the loop: 6 Power Charges → Falling Thunder
  2. Transition to Chaos Inoculation and stack ES
  3. Swap to a dedicated setup for pinnacle bosses
Main skill
Alternate Whirling Assault and Falling Thunder. Store up to 6 Power Charges, then Falling Thunder
Supports
Whirling Assault: Culmination II + Ailith's Chimes converts consumed combo into Power Charges. Falling Thunder: Nova Projectiles I for mapping. In the pinnacle-boss-only setup, remove Nova Projectiles I and other utility. Critical is also supplemented by Charge Regulation, Blindside and Pinpoint Critical.
Passives
Respec into Chaos Inoculation and rebuild ailment and stun thresholds around ES. Add more runes with Runic Meridians. The source states that Abasement and Shimmering Mirage are required to prevent permanent stun / permanent freeze. Martial Adept recovers ES when you consume combo.
Gear priorities
A high-physical + critical strike chance Sinister Quarterstaff. Gloves: Fists of Stone, converted by Way of the Stonefist. Armor: Evasion / ES. Boots: 35% movement speed. Raise Falling Thunder's level with a Prism of Belief.
Replacement candidates
Replace all Life mods with Evasion / ES. On the amulet, aim for max rolls of defensive mods and +levels to melee skills. Push gems to Lv20, 20% quality and 6 links.
Caution
Herald of Ice explosions need Freeze. Secure cold damage through Heart of the Well or the "gain damage as cold" on Fists of Stone. If the big hit does not crit, you have to rebuild your charges from scratch.
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, progress with 3-mod jewels and max-rolling each gear piece.

BUILD PROFILE

Falling Thunder Martial Artist: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • A Power-Charge-spending Falling Thunder hit is huge, and boss damage is high (source strength: High Boss Damage).
  • If you freeze enemies with cold damage, Herald of Ice explosions can process packs (source strength: Herald Explosions).
  • Designed to push critical strike chance close to 100%, so it keeps growing as your gear improves (source strength: Great Scaling).
  • The main gem is Tier 1, so you can start using Falling Thunder from Lv1 (poe2db).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • A melee build, and Act 1 is rough (source weaknesses: Melee / Rough Act 1).
  • Combo and Power Charge management are assumed; there are many buttons and operation is complex (source weakness: Combo Based).
  • There are two respecs during the campaign, at Lv10 and Lv16, and the Lv16 one transitions you to Hollow Palm Technique.
  • The finished form requires Ailith's Chimes; without it, the charge engine does not turn.
  • If the big hit does not crit, you have to rebuild your Power Charges from scratch (source gear explanation).

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon: critical strike chance and damage. During the transition, elemental damage; in the finished form, a high-physical + critical Sinister Quarterstaff
  2. Gloves: added damage to attacks, critical damage, attack speed (converted by Way of the Stonefist). During Hollow Palm, +levels to melee skills
  3. Armor: Evasion / ES (Chaos Inoculation in the finished form). Boots: 35% movement speed. Amulet: +levels to melee skills and critical

FAQ

Falling Thunder Martial Artist build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who want to cycle combos and Power Charges and put out big numbers with a single melee hit

A melee build, and Act 1 is rough (source weaknesses: Melee / Rough Act 1).
