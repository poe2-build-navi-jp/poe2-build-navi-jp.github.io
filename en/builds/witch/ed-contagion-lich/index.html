BUILD PROFILE

ED / Contagion Lich: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • There are per-stage setups from Lv1 to Endgame centered on Contagion and Essence Drain.
  • Written guides explain that it easily combines clearing regular enemy packs with safe positioning.
  • Setups are presented separately for the campaign, T1–T15, full-life and low-life versions.
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) lists as pros 'SSF campaign in about 5 hours, Arbiter in about 10 hours', 'very strong area clear' and 'a large ES pool'.
  • The clear is exhilarating, and you can fight with a safe, ranged playstyle (PoE Vault).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Using multiple skills makes mana consumption heavy.
  • In single-target fights, operations with different roles increase: Bonestorm, Dark Effigy, Despair and more.
  • The low-life version has many requirements, so do not partially mix it in from the early game.
  • It assumes a playstyle that does not get hit (Maxroll's con: 'Don't Get Hit Playstyle').
  • Damage depends heavily on whether you can obtain or craft a decent weapon, and damage over time takes time to take effect (PoE Vault).
  • Single-target fights are mediocre, and scaling and burst damage are modest. Damage depends on the performance of the wand and focus (Gamerch).

Gear priorities

  1. Campaign: + chaos / spell / projectile skill levels on wand, focus and amulet. Gear with Life, ES and resistances, and boots with 20–30% movement speed (Maxroll).
  2. Weapon importance is high: PoE Vault lists as a weakness that damage depends heavily on whether you can obtain or craft a decent weapon. For the wand: + Chaos Spell Skills, Spell Damage, Cast Speed.
  3. After the campaign: update weak slots at shops and gambling vendors, and first secure the resistance caps (Maxroll).
  4. From maps onward: update ES gear, and aim for 100% item rarity early. For jewels, increased maximum ES %, and at least one '% of maximum Mana recovered on Kill' for mana sustain (Maxroll).
  5. Spirit: the Maxroll-derived condition that using Convalescence and Ravenous Swarm together requires 'a total of +50 Spirit on amulet and chest' could not be re-confirmed, as the source guide could not be obtained.
  6. Endgame: + levels to all skills, ES, item rarity, high movement speed boots, Lv20–21 gems with 20% quality. The low-life version equips the unique Atziri's Disdain early after taking Eternal Life (Maxroll).
  7. No unique is mandatory (Maxroll).
