PRACTICAL GUIDE

If you are stuck on minion Spirit shortages or the Spectre switch

Cross-checked against the FAQ and gear direction of Thy's 0.5.5 guide. The dynamic support setups for all stages and actual behavior have not been re-verified.

I am Lv37 but cannot switch to Vaal Guard?

Level alone does not trigger the switch. The source guide targets the grenade-throwing 50 Spirit Vaal Guard after reaching Utzaal in Act 3. Keep the Skeletal Sniper setup until the target and the needed Spirit are in place.

When minion Spirit is short, which gear should I check?

The source guide names the sceptre, amulet and chest as slots that can have Spirit. Compare the needed amount for the summon you want to use against your current gear, and also check minion levels for damage and your own defense.

Powered Zealot — can I switch as soon as I hit Lv67?

The source author gives Lv65–70 as a switch guide, and recommends moving once you have a 5-link and the needed supports. With a 4-link, the author's judgment is to prioritize Vaal Guard. Do not treat level alone as a mandatory condition or a guarantee of the switch.

BUILD PROFILE

Sniper Minion Infernalist: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Per-stage sources are published for each Act, the Interludes, and Lv67–90.
  • Minions handle attacking, so the player can easily focus on watching the boss's movements.
  • Gear goals — minion levels, Spirit and defense — are easy to understand.
  • PoE Vault's leveling version introduces it as a safe, ranged playstyle that attacks almost automatically.
  • About the Endgame Spectre version, PoE Vault introduces it as 'easy to play' with 'very high single-target damage' (a source for 0.5).
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) explicitly states the build works without any unique items.
  • Defense specialized in ES can withstand large hits and recover quickly. PoE Vault says it feels 'very durable'.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Movement speed is slower than high-speed clearing builds, and minion AI can get stuck in narrow places (PoE Vault also lists minion AI problems as a weakness).
  • You need to switch the Spectre setup in Act 3 and around Lv67.
  • Later durability depends on gear updates across multiple areas: ES, block and resistances.
  • Starting Spectre play requires a certain amount of Spirit (PoE Vault).
  • The Lineage support gems used in Endgame are expensive (PoE Vault).

Gear priorities

  1. Sceptre: increased Spirit, increased minion damage, + levels to all minion skills. Check the Rattling Sceptre from early on (PoE Vault).
  2. Off-hand: PoE Vault's Endgame Spectre version uses a Focus (ES, + levels to all minion skills). The Maxroll version uses a high-block shield (ES, Life, resistances).
  3. Helmet and amulet: + levels to all minion skills, ES, Life. Reservation efficiency on the helmet and Spirit on the amulet are also effective (PoE Vault).
  4. Chest: ES gear with Spirit. Gloves: ES, Life, resistances (PoE Vault). Maxroll also guides getting as much ES as possible on chest, boots, helmet, gloves and amulet.
  5. Resistances and attributes: do not fix them to specific slots; meet 75% elemental resistances and required attributes with the total across all gear (Maxroll).
  6. Rings: Life, resistances, minion damage or minion attack / cast speed (PoE Vault).
  7. No unique is mandatory (Maxroll). The boots Bones of Ullr (reservation efficiency for undead minions) are PoE Vault's top-priority target, and Maxroll also introduces them as useful. If you obtain Exalted / Chaos Orbs during the campaign, there is also a plan to spend them on purchases such as Enfolding Dawn (Maxroll).
