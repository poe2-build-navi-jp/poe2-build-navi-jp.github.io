LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Twister Spirit Walker leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 8 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–10You are here
  1. Prepare a spear and the Whirling Slash / Twister gems
  2. Keep Whirling Slash at Lv1 and chain into Twister
  3. Secure boots with movement speed and resistances
Main skill
Once you have a spear and the needed gems, create a whirlwind with Whirling Slash and chain into Twister. Pounce is for movement, Shred is a werewolf-form support candidate; neither is treated as the published route's main skill.
Supports
Early on, prioritize supports that extend physical damage and attack speed; check names you do not have in the source's stage table.
Passives
The source's Early Game stage. Check the route toward projectiles, attack speed and cold.
Gear priorities
Physical damage on the spear, movement speed on boots, Life and resistances on armor.
Replacement candidates
Prioritize replacing a spear whose physical damage is low for your current level, and boots with no movement speed.
Caution
Whirling Slash is used to generate whirlwinds, not for damage, so the source guides keeping it at Lv1.
When to move to the next stage
A progress guide around Lv11. Check your spear upgrade and the basic Whirling Slash → Twister combo.

Lv11–20You are here
  1. Create a whirlwind with Whirling Slash
  2. Launch the whirlwind at enemies with Twister
  3. Prioritize gem updates on the Twister side
Main skill
Create a whirlwind with Whirling Slash and launch it into enemy packs with Twister.
Supports
Prioritize Twister's damage and area. Use War Banner as support once obtained.
Passives
Continue the source's Early Game stage, checking in the order projectiles, attack speed, cold.
Gear priorities
Physical damage on the spear, Life, resistances, Evasion. Keep movement speed on boots.
Replacement candidates
If damage stalls, replace the spear; if defense feels shaky, replace the slots lacking resistances and Life.
Caution
Do not over-level Whirling Slash; prioritize updating the Twister side.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv21. You can reproduce the basic combo of making a whirlwind and launching it with Twister.

Lv21–30You are here
  1. Add Ice-Tipped Arrows to the combo
  2. Set up both weapon sets
  3. Prepare Barrage for single-target fights
Main skill
Whirling Slash → Ice-Tipped Arrows → Twister. If you adopt Barrage, use it before firing the Twister you want to strengthen.
Supports
Prioritize Twister and Ice-Tipped Arrows, and add Barrage for single-target fights in stages.
Passives
The source's Mid Campaign stage. After taking Spirit Walker, check the current route including Primal Bounty.
Gear priorities
Prepare both weapon sets, and check physical damage on the spear, added cold, Life and resistances.
Replacement candidates
First arrange skill assignments and gear requirements so the main skills work correctly on both weapon sets.
Caution
Ascendancy timing varies with progress, so do not apply names ahead of time before you obtain them.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv31. Weapon set switching and the three-skill combo are stable.
Source stage: The source's Mid Campaign stage. After taking Spirit Walker, check the current route including Primal Bounty.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv31–40You are here
  1. Create multiple whirlwinds before attacking
  2. Prioritize updating Twister and Barrage
  3. Check Evasion, Deflect and resistances
Main skill
Use Whirling Slash three or more times, prepare with Ice-Tipped Arrows and Barrage, then fire Twister.
Supports
Prioritize updating Twister, Barrage and Ice-Tipped Arrows, and keep the whirlwind-generating Whirling Slash at Lv1.
Passives
The source's Mid Campaign stage. Extend projectiles, cold and attack speed, and check Evasion and Deflect for defense.
Gear priorities
A high physical damage spear, movement speed, Life, resistances, Evasion.
Replacement candidates
The weapon is top priority, then fix resistance and movement speed shortfalls.
Caution
If the number of whirlwinds and the skill order break down, damage falls, so check the operation in single-target fights.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv41. You can reproduce the combo in both clearing and single-target fights.

BUILD PROFILE

Twister Spirit Walker: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Published sources say you can start leveling with no mandatory uniques.
  • You can create a whirlwind with Whirling Slash and launch it with Twister, chaining into area attacks.
  • The direction for the early game, campaign and Endgame references overseas guides. The numeric level bands are this site's organizational guide, and the sources do not guarantee transitions at each level.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • It requires assigning two weapon sets and multiple skills, and is not a complete one-button setup.
  • You need to practice the order of Whirling Slash, Ice-Tipped Arrows and Twister.
  • Endgame unique candidates are optional, and base gear and resistances must be sorted out first.

Gear priorities

  1. Spear: physical damage, added damage, attack speed
  2. Armor: Life or Energy Shield, Evasion, resistances, Deflect
  3. Support: needed Spirit and movement speed on boots
