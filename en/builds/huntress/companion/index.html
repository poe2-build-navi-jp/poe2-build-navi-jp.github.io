BUILD PROFILE

Companion Spirit Walker: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • There is a clear Endgame plan built around a unique beast as the main damage dealer.
  • The Act 1–3 leveling route and the conditions for switching to the companion setup are explained.
  • Simple operation: Maxroll (0.5.5) introduces it as a very simple playstyle where you can sit back and let your minions do the job.
  • The companion draws enemy attention and soaks attacks, so you can focus on positioning and staying safe (PoE Vault).
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) explicitly states the build works without any unique items. However, it does not explicitly call it SSF-viable, and it notes that the method of pausing with the Marketplace hotkey to check the beast's abilities in maps cannot be used in SSF.
  • Odealo (0.5.0) lists 'easy to play', 'high damage' and 'high survivability' as PROS, and explains that the Mighty Silverfist in Act 3 solves the lack of boss damage.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Tame Beast is a Tier 7 gem, so it cannot be used early on; switching to the companion setup is recommended in Act 3, after the second trial.
  • You need to keep gold aside for the respec from the Twister setup to the companion setup.
  • After the switch, clearing is slower than the early Twister playstyle (PoE Vault).
  • The Endgame version has somewhat low durability early on, and you need to watch your positioning (PoE Vault).
  • Finding the optimal boss companion (an individual with good mods) requires farming. However, this is only about pushing the build to its absolute limit (PoE Vault).
  • A boss companion is poor at clearing large packs, its attacks are slow and its aim is rough. Minion AI can also cause problems (Odealo).

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon for the first half of leveling (until the Act 3 switch): increased / added physical damage, accuracy and critical strike chance on the spear. Gloves and rings take added damage (PoE Vault).
  2. Weapon after the switch: Sceptre + shield. On the sceptre, prioritize '+ levels to minion skills' and 'Allies in your Presence deal increased Damage' (Maxroll 0.5.5).
  3. Helmet and amulet: + levels to minion skills. Start looking for the helmet and sceptre from Act 2 (PoE Vault).
  4. Shield, chest, gloves, boots: maximum Life and resistances. PoE Vault's Endgame version is Evasion-based, and aims for 'gain Deflection equal to a portion of your Evasion' on gloves and boots.
  5. Boots: movement speed.
  6. At the start of mapping: secure 75% elemental resistances (chaos resistance is good to have). When you change gear, do not drop below the resistance caps or required attributes (Maxroll / PoE Vault).
  7. Endgame main hand: with the ascendancy The Catha's Balance, the main-hand weapon's damage is added to the companion, so use the unique Tyranny's Grip (or Trenchtimbre for a more durable option) upgraded with a Verisium Anvil. The build also works without uniques (Maxroll 0.5.5).
  8. Rings and belt: Breach-crafted minion mods are also effective, but meet required attributes and resistances first (PoE Vault).
