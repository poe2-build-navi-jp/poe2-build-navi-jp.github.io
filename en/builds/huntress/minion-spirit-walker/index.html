LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Minion Spirit Walker leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–21 (crossbow leveling)You are here
  1. Clear packs with Flash Grenade
  2. Update to gear with added damage, Life and resistances
  3. Keep gold saved for the respec
Main skill
Progress with a crossbow. Flash Grenade is the leveling mainstay, Explosive Grenade is single-target for bosses you have stopped in place, Frost Bomb supports bosses, and Crossbow Shot fills in as the basic attack
Supports
Plan to attach area-expanding supports / runes to Flash Grenade (per Zizaran's video summary. Check the exact gem setup in the source guide's (Maxroll) planner).
Passives
Use the pre-transition attack tree. Follow the source guide's leveling tree (planner).
Gear priorities
Flat added damage, Life, resistances. Gear can be minimal (Zizaran's video summary).
Replacement candidates
Compare crossbows by added damage first.
Caution
The finished army setup cannot be assembled at this stage. Do not force minions; progress with the crossbow (U4GM summary). Do not spend all the gold for the respec.
When to move to the next stage
Reach around Lv22 and be able to attempt the Trial of the Sekhemas (first ascendancy).
Source stage: Use the pre-transition attack tree. Follow the source guide's leveling tree (planner).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv22–End of Act 2 (transition begins)You are here
  1. Take Wild Protector
  2. Respec passives to minion-based
  3. Look for a helmet and sceptre with + minion skill levels
Main skill
Choose Spirit Walker at the first ascendancy and add the Wild Protector bear spirit to the army. Transition toward minions while still using crossbow attacks
Supports
Plan to give companions Minion Splash, Rage, Rapid Attacks and Hulking Minions type supports (Zizaran's video summary).
Passives
Respec passives from attack-based to minion-based (Switchblade Gaming's guide puts the Lv22 respec as the turning point, at a cost of about 7,000 gold). The first ascendancy point is Wild Protector.
Gear priorities
Start looking for + minion skill levels on helmet and sceptre. Next, minion damage and minion attack speed (U4GM summary).
Replacement candidates
Consider switching the weapon from a crossbow to a sceptre with minion skill levels.
Caution
The bear spirit does not count toward the companion limit (poe2db). Spirit reservation allocation decides how many companions and support skills you can run (U4GM summary).
When to move to the next stage
Advance to Act 3 and be able to take The Natural Order at the second ascendancy.

Act 3–Campaign End (forming the army)You are here
  1. Aim to capture the Mighty Silverfist
  2. Settle the use of Pain Offering and marks
  3. Check the Spirit breakdown
Main skill
With The Natural Order, Tame Beast can capture unique beasts. Build the army around the Mighty Silverfist as the main candidate, with Wolf Pack and Skeletal minions. You support with Pain Offering, Mark for Death and Charged Mark
Supports
Skeletal minions also serve as targets for Pain Offering (U4GM summary). Companion supports continue the plan from the transition stage.
Passives
The second ascendancy point is The Natural Order. The third is The Catha's Balance, where 60% of the main-hand weapon's damage is added to companions (Switchblade Gaming's order).
Gear priorities
Balance + minion skill levels (helmet / sceptre), Spirit, Life and resistances.
Replacement candidates
If Spirit is short, prioritize updating the sceptre and Spirit sources.
Caution
You can take at most 1 Tamed Beast (unique beast) at a time (The Natural Order, poe2db). Obtaining a targeted companion (the Mighty Silverfist or one with a Haste aura) requires farming (a weakness in Zizaran's video summary).
When to move to the next stage
Finish the campaign with the army's lineup in place and enter maps.

Early MapsYou are here
  1. Sort out resistances and Life
  2. Review the companion lineup
  3. Start looking for ES gear
Main skill
Clear with the army. You maintain marks and Pain Offering from a safe position and leave the killing to the army
Supports
Keep the army's support setup, and swap according to the role that is lacking (area / single target).
Passives
Extend minion damage and companion nodes, and start building the defense base (evasion / ES).
Gear priorities
Minion skill levels, minion damage, minion attack speed, Spirit (the Endgame priorities in the U4GM summary).
Replacement candidates
If you get killed, update armor and resistances; if damage is lacking, update slots with + minion skill levels.
Caution
Early defense tends to be unstable. There are points where a patchwork of armor, evasion and block is needed (a weakness in Zizaran's video summary).
When to move to the next stage
The army is stable in high-Tier maps and you start gathering ES gear.

Continue strengthening the army. Work on companion selection (beasts with auras and the like) with the finished ascendancy in view
Supports

Endgame (ES / Chaos Inoculation)You are here
  1. Resolve attribute shortfalls
  2. Upgrade jewels within budget
  3. Continue selecting companion mods
Main skill
Handle high-Tier maps and bosses with an army centered on the Mighty Silverfist
Supports
The source guide's (Maxroll) Endgame planner setup is authoritative for companion and Skeletal supports. The summary sources could not confirm individual gems.
Passives
Take Chaos Inoculation. Finish defense around ES and evasion.
Gear priorities
Maximize minion skill levels, and upgrade jewels according to budget (U4GM summary. Expensive ones can wait).
Replacement candidates
Life stats are no longer needed. Update one piece at a time based on ES and minion stats.
Caution
Strict attribute requirements are the biggest hurdle (a weakness in Zizaran's video summary). There is no way to recall minions, and companion positioning inconveniences remain (same source). There are reports that with certain Tablet mods, when a unique monster gains rare mods, the companion's critical strike chance grows close to the cap (same source).
Completion condition
After completion, extend the build through companion selection and jewels.

BUILD PROFILE

Minion Spirit Walker: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • The army handles combat, so you can focus on positioning and support (Switchblade Gaming's guide states 'The companions do the killing').
  • Companions serve as both tank and damage, smoothly handling both clear and single target (Zizaran's video summary, Exile Codex).
  • Leveling is cheap and stable. Early gear only needs added damage, Life and resistances (same source).
  • Huge scaling potential. There are reports of one-shotting most content and three-shotting bosses (same source).
  • Listed as A Tier in Maxroll's League Starter Tier List (updated November 3, 2026), and a setup Zizaran adopted as his own league starter.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • The main army setup cannot be used from the start. You level with a crossbow (grenades) until Lv22, and need gold for the passive respec (U4GM summary / Zizaran's video summary).
  • Attribute requirements are strict, and you can easily end up chasing stats (a weakness in Zizaran's video summary).
  • Early defense is unstable, and there are points where you need a patchwork of armour, evasion and block (same source).
  • Obtaining specific companions (the Mighty Silverfist, beasts with a Haste aura) requires targeted farming (same source).
  • There is no way to recall minions (a Convocation equivalent), leaving positioning inconveniences (same source).
  • Whether it works in SSF is not stated in the sources.

Gear priorities

  1. Sceptre: + minion skill levels and Spirit. The base for the army's numbers and damage (U4GM summary).
  2. Helmet: + minion skill levels. Look for it with top priority alongside the sceptre (U4GM summary).
  3. Minion damage and minion attack speed: stack them across all gear (U4GM summary).
  4. Spirit: decides how many companions and support skills you can run at once. Calculate the reservation allocation first (U4GM summary).
  5. Defense: early on, added damage, Life and resistances (Zizaran's video summary). Later, grow ES and prepare for the Chaos Inoculation transition (U4GM summary).
  6. Jewels: leave special jewel upgrades for later, within what the budget allows (U4GM summary).
