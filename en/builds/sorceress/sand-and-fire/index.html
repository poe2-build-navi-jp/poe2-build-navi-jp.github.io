BUILD PROFILE

Sand and Fire Varashta: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Stage-by-stage guides from Lv1 through the campaign to Endgame are published.
  • After taking the first ascendancy, you can move to Kelari's command skills (PoE Vault recommends taking it at Lv21–22).
  • Maxroll (0.5.5) lists "very tanky" and "very high damage" as strengths.
  • Djinn are not killed, so you do not need to invest in minion survivability. The command skills' mana costs are also very low (PoE Vault).
  • The passive Archon of Undeath can greatly shorten the command skills' cooldowns, making bosses easy to beat by spamming (Gamerch).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Until you take the first ascendancy, the build is not in its intended form.
  • You must manage command skill cooldowns and multiple actions. PoE Vault calls it "high APM (actions per minute), with a feel unlike a typical summoning build," and Maxroll also says it "can be button intensive."
  • Djinn attacks do not have a wide area, so scattered enemies take effort to handle (PoE Vault).
  • Maxroll lists minion-related issues and visual clarity as weaknesses.

Gear priorities

  1. Weapon: a sceptre is mandatory. Prioritize +minion skill levels, Spirit, "increased damage for nearby allies" and "increased critical damage bonus for nearby allies" (PoE Vault).
  2. Off-hand: a focus is recommended. A shield is about equal defensively (PoE Vault).
  3. Armor: during the campaign, Armour, Life and missing resistances (original guide). PoE Vault emphasizes ES, and Maxroll's Endgame setup stacks ES, ES recovery and block chance. Time the ES transition to the source's stages.
  4. Resistances: runes prioritize maintaining the resistance caps above all (PoE Vault).
  5. From Act 4 onward: look for Spirit, ES and +minion skill levels (PoE Vault). Boots take movement speed.
  6. No unique is mandatory (Maxroll). Enfolding Dawn (best when upgraded with Runeforge) and Bones of Ullr are useful. Defensively, a Lv36 Soul Mantle and the Lv51 Waveshaper are also candidates (Maxroll).
  7. The required attributes for the setup switch.
