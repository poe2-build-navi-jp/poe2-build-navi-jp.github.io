PoE2 Frostbolt + Cast on Critical Comet Disciple of Varashta Build | 0.5.5 Leveling Guide

Updated: 2026-11-26

Sorceress / Disciple of Varashta

Main skill: Frostbolt — Snakepit (left ring) turns its piercing into forking, and the overlapping explosions clear the screen. Shatter Frost Wall's ice crystals with Frostbolt to fire Comets in rapid succession through Cast on Elemental Ailment / Cast on Critical. Before you have Snakepit, level with Ice Nova (clear) and Frost Darts (single target), and switch to Frostbolt once you can equip Snakepit at Lv32. The Disciple of Varashta ascendancy Instruments of Power lets you use a staff and focus together, and Baryanic Leylines widens the radius of Time-Lost Sapphires.

LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Frostbolt + Cast on Critical Comet Disciple of Varashta leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 6 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Act 1 (Ice Nova + Frost Darts leveling)You are here
  1. Freeze enemies with Ice Nova as you advance
  2. Chip down single targets with Frost Darts
  3. Create Cold Infusions with Frost Bomb and Snap
Main skill
Clear with Ice Nova and chip down single targets with Frost Darts. Use Frost Bomb and Snap to create Cold Infusion Remnants and strengthen Ice Nova and Frost Darts. Movement is Pounce, and the persistent buff is Arctic Armour. Advance with Spark to the Clearfell Encampment, and engrave Ice Nova and Frost Bomb from Renly and the Mysterious Clearfell Encampment (source).
Supports
Ice Nova: Controlled Destruction + Ice Bite. Frost Darts: Controlled Destruction + Rapid Casting. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Cascade. Snap: Magnified Area + Controlled Destruction.
Passives
114 nodes in the campaign. Raise the Infusion holding cap with Frozen Limit and Infused Limits (source FAQ). On the way, take efficient cold damage and Life nodes.
Gear priorities
Magic / rare gear with plenty of Life and resistances. A wand + focus with cold spell damage. Boots with movement speed (source FAQ defense guidance).
Replacement candidates
Frostbolt cannot be used without Snakepit, so do not equip it yet (source FAQ). Update gear with Life + resistances first.
Caution
Frostbolt does not function without Snakepit. Ice Nova and Frost Darts lose a lot of damage without a Cold Infusion, so collect Remnants with Frost Bomb and Snap (source).
When to move to the next stage
Enter Act 2 and add Elemental Weakness.
Source stage: 114 nodes in the campaign. Raise the Infusion holding cap with Frozen Limit and Infused Limits (source FAQ). On the way, take efficient cold damage and Life nodes.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Act 2 (adding a curse)You are here
  1. Lower enemy resistances with Elemental Weakness
  2. Strengthen Ice Nova with Considered Casting
  3. Keep creating Cold Infusions with Frost Bomb
Main skill
Continue leveling with Ice Nova + Frost Darts and lower enemy resistances with Elemental Weakness. Update Frost Bomb's support to Spell Echo.
Supports
Ice Nova: Controlled Destruction + Considered Casting + Ice Bite. Frost Darts: Controlled Destruction + Considered Casting. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Echo. Snap: Magnified Area + Controlled Destruction. Elemental Weakness: Heightened Curse + Efficiency.
Passives
To 114 nodes in the campaign. Advance toward cold damage and critical strikes, preparing for Frost Wall being added in Act 3 (source planner).
Gear priorities
Keep prioritizing Life + resistances. From Act 4, start switching to high-ES Intelligence gear (source FAQ).
Replacement candidates
Update gear with Life + resistances first. ES gear comes from Act 4 (source FAQ).
Caution
There are few defensive tools in Acts 1–3. Raise Life and resistances, and use Frost Wall defensively as well (source FAQ).
When to move to the next stage
Enter Act 3 and add Frost Wall and Siphon Elements.
Source stage: To 114 nodes in the campaign. Advance toward cold damage and critical strikes, preparing for Frost Wall being added in Act 3 (source planner).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Act 3–Maps (adding Frost Wall)You are here
  1. Create ice crystals with Frost Wall
  2. Collect Infusions with Siphon Elements
  3. Add Cold Penetration to Frost Darts
Main skill
Create ice crystals with Frost Wall, freeze enemies with Siphon Elements and collect Cold Infusions. Add Elemental Focus and Cold Penetration to Frost Darts to raise single-target damage.
Supports
Ice Nova: Considered Casting + Astral Projection + Ice Bite II. Frost Darts: Considered Casting + Elemental Focus + Cold Penetration. Frost Wall: Icicle + Spell Cascade + Cooldown Recovery. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Echo + Cooldown Recovery. Snap: Magnified Area II + Elemental Focus + Cooldown Recovery. Elemental Weakness: Heightened Curse + Efficiency + Prolonged Duration II. Siphon Elements: Harmonic Remnants II + Remnant Potency.
Passives
114 nodes in the campaign. Scale cold damage, critical strikes and Life + ES (source planner).
Gear priorities
Start switching to high-ES Intelligence gear. Maintain a Life + ES hybrid (source).
Replacement candidates
Gradually update to Life + ES hybrid gear (source).
Caution
Infusions stack up to 3 at most. Frozen Limit and Infused Limits can raise the cap (source FAQ).
When to move to the next stage
Clear the campaign and enter Maps. Add Cast on Elemental Ailment + Comet.

Maps (starting Cast on Elemental Ailment + Comet)You are here
  1. Drop Comets with Cast on Elemental Ailment
  2. Freeze enemies to build Comet energy
  3. Add extra damage with Living Bomb
Main skill
At Lv58, put Comet and Living Bomb into Cast on Elemental Ailment so Comets fall automatically when you freeze. Comets are strengthened by Fire Remnants, and Living Bomb automatically creates Fire Remnants. Freeze enemies with Ice Nova and shatter Frost Wall crystals to build energy. For single target, debuff with Sigil of Power and Elemental Weakness before attacking.
Supports
Ice Nova: Astral Projection + Magnified Area II + Ice Bite II + Rising Tempest. Frost Darts: Elemental Focus + Considered Casting + Cold Penetration + Cold Mastery. Frost Wall: Spell Cascade + Icicle + Fortress + Cooldown Recovery II. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Echo + Cooldown Recovery II + Potent Exposure. Snap: Magnified Area II + Cooldown Recovery II + Elemental Focus + Innervate. Cast on Elemental Ailment: Comet + Living Bomb + Efficiency II + Concentrated Area. Elemental Weakness: Heightened Curse + Efficiency II + Focused Curse + Rapid Casting II. Sigil of Power: Prolonged Duration II + Cooldown Recovery II + Magnified Area II. Siphon Elements: Harmonic Remnants II + Remnant Potency III.
Passives
141 nodes in Early Maps. Take Careful Assassin, Controlling Magic and Critical Exploit, preparing for the switch to Cast on Critical (source). The goal is 70%+ critical strike chance on Frostbolt.
Gear priorities
Mana: prepare two jewels with 2% of maximum mana recovered on kill (source FAQ). Update the staff + focus to prioritize cold spell levels + critical strike chance (source summary). Secure 30+ Spirit on the chest or amulet (source).
Replacement candidates
Insert the two mana recovery jewels. Update the staff + focus to prioritize cold spell levels + critical strike chance (source).
Caution
Each Comet needs 300 energy. A regular monster's critical strike only generates 1 energy, so Comets will not cycle if there are too few frozen enemies (source FAQ). Bosses only freeze once every few seconds, so single-target damage is low with Cast on Elemental Ailment (source).
When to move to the next stage
Once Frostbolt's critical strike chance reaches around 70%, switch from Cast on Elemental Ailment to Cast on Critical and proceed to the Frostbolt swap (source). Snakepit can be equipped at Lv32.
Source stage: 141 nodes in Early Maps. Take Careful Assassin, Controlling Magic and Critical Exploit, preparing for the switch to Cast on Critical (source). The goal is 70%+ critical strike chance on Frostbolt.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Frostbolt Swap (Lv32+)You are here
  1. Equip the forking ring in the left ring and switch to Frostbolt
  2. Shatter Frost Wall's ice crystals with Frostbolt
  3. Add a recovery tool with Convalescence
Main skill
Switch to Frostbolt. Snakepit turns piercing into forking, and the overlapping explosions clear the screen. Shatter Frost Wall's ice crystals with Frostbolt and fire Comets in rapid succession through Cast on Critical. Raise Comet to Lv21. Add Convalescence as a recovery tool.
Supports
Frostbolt: Projectile Acceleration III + Multishot II + Pinpoint Critical + Ice Bite II. Cast on Critical: Comet Lv21 + Living Bomb Lv3 + Efficiency II + Pinpoint Critical. Frost Wall: Spell Cascade + Fortress + Cooldown Recovery II + Pinpoint Critical. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Echo + Potent Exposure + Cooldown Recovery II. Elemental Weakness: Efficiency II + Prolonged Duration II + Heightened Curse + Cursed Ground. Convalescence: Second Wind III + Prolonged Duration II + Cooldown Recovery II. Sigil of Power: Prolonged Duration II + Cooldown Recovery II + Magnified Area II.
Passives
144 nodes with Frostbolt + CI. Scale ES in preparation for the move to Chaos Inoculation (source planner).
Gear priorities
Equip Snakepit in the left ring (the forking core). For the staff + focus, prioritize cold spell levels and critical strike chance (source summary). With Maligaro's Virtuosity gloves you can avoid the Pinpoint Critical penalty (source).
Replacement candidates
Replace the left ring with Snakepit (the core). Maintain a Life + ES hybrid until the CI transition (source).
Caution
Snakepit is limited to the left ring slot. If a Vaal Cultivation Orb removes the forking effect, it reverts to piercing (source FAQ). Forked projectiles exploding can look like piercing, but that is normal behavior (source FAQ).
When to move to the next stage
Once your ES gear is ready and ES reaches 5,000–6,000, move to Chaos Inoculation and proceed to Endgame (source).
Source stage: 144 nodes with Frostbolt + CI. Scale ES in preparation for the move to Chaos Inoculation (source planner).View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (CI + min-maxed)You are here
  1. Move to Chaos Inoculation
  2. Add Frostbolt projectiles with Wildshards II
  3. Get two Sapphire jewels that raise area and critical strike chance
Main skill
Move to Chaos Inoculation and fight with Life 1 and ES specialization — the "blue Life" style. Frostbolt remains the core. Once you take Baryanic Leylines, equip two Time-Lost Sapphires. Supplement mana sustain with Mana Remnants.
Supports
Frostbolt: Wildshards II + Vilenta's Propulsion + Pinpoint Critical + Efficiency II + Ice Bite II. Cast on Critical: Comet Lv21 + Living Bomb Lv3 + Efficiency II + Pinpoint Critical + Concentrated Area. Frost Wall: Spell Cascade + Fortress + Cooldown Recovery II + Rapid Casting II + Pinpoint Critical. Frost Bomb: Overabundance + Spell Echo + Potent Exposure + Cooldown Recovery II. Elemental Weakness: Heightened Curse + Prolonged Duration II + Efficiency II + Cursed Ground + Ritualistic Curse. Convalescence: Prolonged Duration II + Second Wind III + Cooldown Recovery II. Mana Remnants: Harmonic Remnants II + Remnant Potency. Sigil of Power: Magnified Area II + Prolonged Duration II + Cooldown Recovery II.
Passives
152 nodes when min-maxed. Fill in Baryanic Leylines and many ES and critical nodes (source planner and summary). Allocate unconnected passives around a keystone with From Nothing.
Gear priorities
Mageblood (belt), Rite of Passage (spirit: Owl), From Nothing (a jewel that lets you allocate unconnected passives around a keystone), Time-Lost Sapphire (area + critical strike chance) (source planner, Min Maxed). Move to the CI swap gear (ES specialization; chaos resistance is no longer needed). Note that the chest's "Soul Mantle" is the name of a rare item, not the Soul Mantle unique (source planner).
Replacement candidates
Move to the CI swap gear (ES specialization; chaos resistance is no longer needed). Note that the chest's "Soul Mantle" is the name of a rare item, not the Soul Mantle unique (source planner). Min-maxed gear is a long-term goal (source).
Caution
The planner's "Wildshards III" does not exist in the game (only I and II). This draft uses Wildshards II, the highest tier (see the research report). Min-maxed gear is a long-term goal collected over most of a league (per the source guide).
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, progress by refining the Time-Lost Sapphires and max-rolling each gear piece.

BUILD PROFILE

Frostbolt + Cast on Critical Comet Disciple of Varashta: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • You can stop enemies almost completely with Freeze (source strength: Freezes everything). You control the screen with Frostbolt explosions and Comets.
  • High single-target damage (source strength: High single target damage). Shatter Frost Wall's ice crystals with Frostbolt and fire Comets in rapid succession through Cast on Critical.
  • Easy to play once you start using Frostbolt (source strength: Easy to play once using Frostbolt).
  • Defense can move from a Life + ES hybrid to Chaos Inoculation, making gear easy to assemble (source). It nullifies chaos damage and bleeding, and you no longer need chaos resistance.

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Frostbolt cannot be used early (source weakness: Can't use it early). Level with Ice Nova + Frost Darts until you can equip Snakepit at Lv32.
  • Relies on Infusion Remnants during the campaign (source weakness: Relies on Infusion Remnants during the Campaign). Ice Nova and Frost Darts deal little damage without a Cold Infusion.
  • Each Comet needs 300 energy, and a regular monster only generates 1 energy per critical strike (source FAQ). Comets will not cycle if there are too few frozen enemies.
  • Mana is always tight in boss fights (source FAQ). You need investment in mana regeneration rate, maximum mana, flask recovery and cost reduction.
  • There are few defensive tools in Acts 1–3 (source FAQ). The focus is on raising Life and resistances, switching to ES gear from Act 4.

Gear priorities

  1. Snakepit (left ring): the core unique. It turns Frostbolt's piercing into forking so the explosions overlap and clear. Equippable at Lv32 (source).
  2. Staff + Focus: Instruments of Power lets you use a focus alongside a staff (the focus's bonuses are reduced by 50%, but +1 to all spell levels stays +1). Scale cold spell levels and critical strike chance (source summary). The main weapon goal is the Chiming Staff (source).
  3. Defense: a Life + ES hybrid early, later moving to Chaos Inoculation. Move to high-ES Intelligence gear (source). Move to CI at 5,000–6,000 ES (source).
  4. Maligaro's Virtuosity (gloves): not mandatory but strongly recommended. It fixes critical damage at 250% and avoids the Pinpoint Critical penalty (source).
  5. Time-Lost Sapphire: prioritize area-of-effect and critical strike chance mods. Baryanic Leylines widens its radius (source summary).
  6. Mana: sustain it with two jewels with 2% of maximum mana recovered on kill and Mana Remnants (source FAQ).
  7. Spirit: secure Spirit skills such as Cast on Elemental Ailment + Comet and Siphon Elements. At 160 Spirit you can use Mana Remnants (source planner).

FAQ

Frostbolt + Cast on Critical Comet Disciple of Varashta build FAQ

Who is this build for?

Players who like freezing enemies in place while controlling the screen with Frostbolt explosions and automatically falling Comets. For players who like a Disciple of Varashta build that uses a staff + focus through Instruments of Power and makes use of Time-Lost Sapphires.

What weakness should I know before starting?

Frostbolt cannot be used early (source weakness: Can't use it early). Level with Ice Nova + Frost Darts until you can equip Snakepit at Lv32.
