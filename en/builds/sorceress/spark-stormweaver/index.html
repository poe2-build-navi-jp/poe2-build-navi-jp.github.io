LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Spark Stormweaver leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 8 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–10You are here
  1. Put Spark on your main attack button
  2. Create Frost Bomb and use it together with Spark
  3. Look for a staff with spell skill levels
Main skill
Make Spark, obtained from the gem chest at the starting point, your main skill. Your second skill is Frost Bomb, followed by Flame Wall.
Supports
Strengthen Spark as the top priority. Once you create Orb of Storms, add Overabundance I and Unleash so you can place two at once.
Passives
Take the route toward elemental damage, and put attribute nodes mainly into Intelligence. Only supplement the Strength and Dexterity needed for gear and gems.
Gear priorities
Lightning and all spell skill levels on the staff, then cast speed and spell damage. Armor is gear with Energy Shield.
Replacement candidates
Replace staves with spell skill levels as the top priority. Merchant stock changes with level, so check every time you return to town.
Caution
This level band applies the source's "Act 1" content. The correspondence between level and progress is a guideline.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv11. You have obtained Spark, Frost Bomb and Flame Wall, and can defeat packs of enemies with Spark.
Source stage: Take the route toward elemental damage, and put attribute nodes mainly into Intelligence. Only supplement the Strength and Dexterity needed for gear and gems.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv11–20You are here
  1. Create Orb of Storms and place two of them
  2. Add Rising Tempest to Spark
  3. Activate Mana Remnants with 30 Spirit
Main skill
Spark + Frost Bomb. Create Orb of Storms with your first Lv3 Uncut Skill Gem, and cast Spark from within its area.
Supports
From Act 2, Lv2 Uncut Support Gems appear. Add Rising Tempest to Spark, then to Orb of Storms.
Passives
While scaling elemental damage, aim for Secrets of the Orb, which lets you place three Orb of Storms.
Gear priorities
Skill levels and added damage on the staff, Life and resistances on armor, mana recovery rate.
Replacement candidates
Once you obtain 30 Spirit (defeating The King in the Mists in Act 1), activate Mana Remnants.
Caution
Orb of Storms fires chaining lightning at enemies when you cast from within its area, so do not stray too far from where you placed it.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv21. You can place two Orb of Storms and fight in the order Frost Bomb → Orb of Storms → Spark.
Source stage: While scaling elemental damage, aim for Secrets of the Orb, which lets you place three Orb of Storms.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv21–30You are here
  1. Finish the trial and take Storm's Recollection
  2. Open Spark's third socket
  3. Use Elemental Weakness on bosses
Main skill
Spam Frost Bomb → Orb of Storms → Spark. On bosses, use Elemental Weakness (a Lv7 Uncut Skill Gem).
Supports
With the Lesser Jeweller's Orb dropped by enemies around Lv25, open the third socket of Spark, then of Orb of Storms.
Passives
After finishing Act 2's trial (Trial of the Sekhemas), take the Stormweaver's Storm's Recollection. A single Frost Bomb can create 6 Cold Infusions.
Gear priorities
The staff is the top priority. Armor in the order Life, resistances, Energy Shield, mana recovery rate.
Replacement candidates
Optionally, hold a Chiming Staff (Lv25+) in Weapon Set 2 and use Sigil of Power. Set the other skills to Weapon Set 1 only.
Caution
The source states that the Chiming Staff (damage) and Wolf Form (movement speed) cannot be used at the same time — only one of them.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv31. You have taken the ascendancy and can use Elemental Weakness and Frost Bomb in boss fights.
Source stage: After finishing Act 2's trial (Trial of the Sekhemas), take the Stormweaver's Storm's Recollection. A single Frost Bomb can create 6 Cold Infusions.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv31–40You are here
  1. Raise Spark's supports to Zenith II
  2. Switch to Blasphemy at 60 Spirit
  3. Finish the Trial of Chaos and gain 2 more ascendancy points
Main skill
Centered on Spark. On bosses, the order is Sigil of Power → Elemental Weakness → Frost Bomb → Orb of Storms → Spark.
Supports
From Act 3, Lv3 Uncut Support Gems appear. Raise Spark's supports to Zenith II.
Passives
At Act 3's Temple of Chaos, finish Ultimatum (the Trial of Chaos) and obtain 2 ascendancy points.
Gear priorities
Skill levels, cast speed and spell damage on the staff. Armor takes resistances and Energy Shield.
Replacement candidates
With 60 Spirit (The King in the Mists + Act 3's Ignagduk), replace Mana Remnants with Blasphemy and move Elemental Weakness into that socket.
Caution
The source guides you to keep mana above 90% while using Zenith. Keep your remaining mana up with mana recovery rate on the staff, rings and amulet, and with a mana flask.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv41. Elemental Weakness is automated through Blasphemy, and you can reproduce the boss rotation.
Source stage: At Act 3's Temple of Chaos, finish Ultimatum (the Trial of Chaos) and obtain 2 ascendancy points.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv41–Campaign EndYou are here
  1. Add Considered Casting to Spark
  2. Aim to update to a Voltaic Staff
  3. Add Siphon Elements at 100 Spirit
Main skill
Spark as the main skill, supplementing boss damage with Frost Bomb, Orb of Storms and Flame Wall.
Supports
From Act 4, Lv4 Uncut Support Gems appear. Add Considered Casting and Projectile Acceleration II to Spark.
Passives
The second ascendancy is Constant Gale (permanent Arcane Surge). Advance the leveling tree until the campaign ends.
Gear priorities
A Voltaic Staff (item level 36+ gives +3–4 levels to lightning spell skills) has the most room to grow. Maintain resistances and Energy Shield as well.
Replacement candidates
With 100 Spirit (the final reward from defeating Lythara in the Interlude), add Siphon Elements and Clarity I.
Caution
The Greater Jeweller's Orb rarely drops, so do not count on it. If one drops, use it on Spark, then on Orb of Storms.
When to move to the next stage
Campaign complete. Resistances are sorted out, and you can defeat regular enemies and bosses centered on Spark.
Source stage: The second ascendancy is Constant Gale (permanent Arcane Surge). Advance the leveling tree until the campaign ends.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early MapsYou are here
  1. Sort out resistances and Energy Shield
  2. Cover mana shortfalls with Efficiency II
  3. Secure an amulet with Spirit
Main skill
Spam Spark against regular enemies. Against strong enemies, use the order Sigil of Power → Frost Bomb → Orb of Storms → Spark.
Supports
Maintain the Spark and Orb of Storms links, and add Efficiency II to mana-hungry skills to cover mana shortfalls.
Passives
Move from the leveling tree to the Endgame tree. Check Cold Infusions, elemental damage and Energy Shield.
Gear priorities
Secure Energy Shield on the tiara, robe, gloves and sandals. Prefixes take Life and added Energy Shield, and resistances are supplemented with runes.
Replacement candidates
Update one piece at a time in the order: insufficient resistances, insufficient defense, insufficient staff damage. Keep an amulet with Spirit, as you will use it later.
Caution
If you use Mana Tempest, leave its area as soon as you have gained the effect. Staying inside drains your mana rapidly.
When to move to the next stage
You can farm centered on Spark while maintaining resistances in low-tier maps.
Source stage: Move from the leveling tree to the Endgame tree. Check Cold Infusions, elemental damage and Energy Shield.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Early EndgameYou are here
  1. Raise Spirit to 121 or more
  2. Add Cast on Critical and Comet
  3. Raise the critical strike chance with Pinpoint Critical
Main skill
With 121+ Spirit, add Cast on Critical + Comet. Spark's critical strikes automatically trigger Comet, and the controls stay the same as before.
Supports
Add Pinpoint Critical to Spark, Cast on Critical and Orb of Storms to raise the critical strike chance.
Passives
Taking Elemental Equilibrium makes Orb of Storms produce Cold Infusions and Frost Bomb produce Fire Infusions, strengthening Comet.
Gear priorities
Spirit (staff and amulet, the robe prefix, a helmet corruption, side quests) and critical strike chance.
Replacement candidates
The source states the Maligaro's Virtuosity gloves are the key to its damage. After equipping Call of the Brotherhood, prioritize added cold damage over added lightning damage.
Caution
Pinpoint Critical lowers critical damage, so the source assumes it is used together with Maligaro's Virtuosity (which fixes the critical multiplier).
When to move to the next stage
Comet triggers automatically, and you have enough Spirit including Convalescence for emergencies.
Source stage: Taking Elemental Equilibrium makes Orb of Storms produce Cold Infusions and Frost Bomb produce Fire Infusions, strengthening Comet.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Endgame (finished form)You are here
  1. Aim for 5,000+ maximum mana
  2. Keep Spark's critical strike chance at 90%+
  3. Re-check the supported-version display before moving to 1.0
Main skill
The option of rebuilding into a separate high-budget mana-stacking setup. Farming is Spark; bosses are Frost Bomb → Spark. Comet triggers automatically through Cast on Critical.
Supports
Keep Spark's critical strike chance at 90% or more. Strengthen supports in the priority order of the source's gem table, and also raise each skill's quality.
Passives
The ascendancy is Constant Gale, Force of Will, Tempest Caller and Multiplying Squalls. Jewels prioritize critical strike chance, critical damage and spell damage.
Gear priorities
Maximum mana (5,000+ is the guideline), critical strike chance, Spirit. If you do not have an Absent Amulet (with Cast on Critical), supplement Spirit with a sceptre.
Replacement candidates
Cloak of Defiance is not mandatory. If you use a regular Energy Shield chest instead, take Mind Over Matter in the passives.
Caution
This is a different author, gear set, passive tree and ascendancy from the preceding setup that uses Maligaro's Virtuosity. You cannot assume you can transition just by adding mana. Cross-check the required respec, gems and gear together. The source explicitly states it includes expensive gear and is not for a low-budget start. Unconfirmed in 1.0.
Completion condition
The 0.5.5 stage you are using is complete, and you can compare before and after the change in-game.
Source stage: The ascendancy is Constant Gale, Force of Will, Tempest Caller and Multiplying Squalls. Jewels prioritize critical strike chance, critical damage and spell damage.View this stage's passive tree in the source

BUILD PROFILE

Spark Stormweaver: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • The Sorceress can obtain Spark from the gem chest at the starting point and keep using the same main skill from Lv1 through Endgame.
  • Normal farming centers on spamming Spark, and in Endgame Comet automatically triggers on critical strikes (the source describes it as "almost one button").
  • The campaign leveling guide explicitly states that it also works in SSF (no trade).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Damage depends heavily on the staff's performance, according to the leveling guide's weakness column.
  • Boss fights require a multi-button rotation using Frost Bomb, Orb of Storms, Flame Wall and more in sequence.
  • In the overseas guide setup that adds CoC Comet, 121+ Spirit is the guideline. The separate mana-stacking finished form requires 90%+ critical strike chance, 5,000+ maximum mana and a rebuild of gear and tree, making it hard to assemble on a low budget.

Gear priorities

  1. Staff: lightning / all spell skill levels, added elemental damage, increased spell damage, cast speed
  2. Armor: Life, resistances, Energy Shield, mana recovery rate (tiara, robe, gloves, sandals)
  3. Endgame: 121+ Spirit and critical strike chance. The source states the Maligaro's Virtuosity gloves are the key to its damage.
