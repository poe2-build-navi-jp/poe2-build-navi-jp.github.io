LEVELING ROADMAP

PoE2 Shield Wall Warbringer leveling roadmap: Lv1 to Endgame

Only the stage for your current level opens first. All 3 stages are on this page, so you can also check the steps ahead. The level bands are this site's guide to progression. Do not switch on level alone — follow the source's Act, gear, gem and ascendancy conditions first.

Lv1–22 (two-handed mace leveling)You are here
  1. Progress with Rolling Slam and Boneshatter on a two-handed mace until Lv22
  2. Save gold for the transition cost and shield gambling
  3. Take Answered Call with the first ascendancy
Main skill
Rolling Slam and Boneshatter are the core. Perfect Strike for single target.
Supports
Normal attack: Rapid Attacks II + Concentrated Area. Rolling Slam: Brink I. Boneshatter: Impact Shockwave + Magnified Area I. Perfect Strike: Window of Opportunity I + Rapid Attacks I. Infernal Cry: Tireless. Also use Herald of Ash. Setup from the source planner (Level 1-22).
Passives
Take enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements, and put the rest into Strength to grow Life. First ascendancy is Answered Call, which houses an ancestral spirit in your totems for buffs.
Gear priorities
A two-handed mace and armour pieces with Life / resistances / Armour. Save gold for the Lv22 transition and for gambling a shield.
Replacement candidates
Upgrade the two-handed mace with whatever drops. The shield is not used until the Lv22 transition.
Caution
Slow animations early on are a weakness in the source. Shield Wall is not available yet, so this is a stretch where you push through on two-handed mace damage.
When to move to the next stage
Reach Lv22 and secure a level 7 Uncut Skill Gem. Gamble a shield with gold and prepare a high-Armour shield.
Source stage: Take enough attributes to meet gear and gem requirements, and put the rest into Strength to grow Life. First ascendancy is Answered Call, which houses an ancestral spirit in your totems for buffs.View this stage's passive tree in the source
Lv22–Campaign End (switch to Shield Wall)You are here
  1. At Lv22, gamble a high-Armour shield and switch to Shield Wall
  2. Once Fortifying Cry is available, switch to Warcaller's Bellow
  3. Get used to the rotation of alternating Shield Wall and warcries
Main skill
Detonate Shield Wall's fissures with Fortifying Cry or Infernal Cry. Shockwave Totem provides supplementary damage.
Supports
Shield Wall: Rapid Attacks I + Fire Infusion + Magnified Area I. Fortifying Cry: Brutality II + Raging Cry. Infernal Cry: Tireless + Raging Cry. Shockwave Totem: Urgent Totems I. Leap Slam / Shield Charge: Rapid Attacks I. In the late-campaign planner, Shield Wall gains Impale and supports are upgraded to higher tiers such as Magnified Area II.
Passives
Once Fortifying Cry becomes available, switch from Answered Call to Warcaller's Bellow. Warcries lose their cooldown, so you can detonate fissures at any time, and nearby corpses also explode. Allocate Weapon Set 2 passives exclusively for warcries.
Gear priorities
Always look for the shield with the highest Armour. Weapon damage is irrelevant to your damage. Chest with Life and Armour; aim for 75% resistances.
Replacement candidates
Replace the shield immediately whenever one with higher Armour appears. Swap the two-handed mace for a one-handed mace + shield.
Caution
If mana is tight, one source of mana leech on a ring or gloves solves it. Do not level the Leap Slam / Infernal Cry / Shield Charge gems (instruction in the source FAQ).
When to move to the next stage
Finish the campaign and have a clear path to obtaining the Ahn's Citadel and Kaom's Madness lineage supports.

Endgame (branching fissures with lineage supports)You are here
  1. Obtain both lineage supports and branch the fissures
  2. On bosses, fully break Armour before using Sunder
  3. Keep updating the Armour of your shield and chest
Main skill
Detonate the fissures of a Shield Wall branched by Ahn's Citadel and Kaom's Madness with Fortifying Cry / Infernal Cry / Sunder / movement skills.
Supports
Shield Wall: Rapid Attacks II + Ahn's Citadel + Kaom's Madness + Heft + Rageforged II. Fortifying Cry: Raging Cry + Heavy Swing + Brutality III + Atalui's Bloodletting + Heft. Sunder: Rapid Attacks III + Rigwald's Ferocity + Rage III + Prolonged Duration II. War Banner: Daresso's Passion + Magnified Area II + Prolonged Duration II. Scavenged Plating and Tame Beast are also included in the source planner.
Passives
With Anvil's Weight, make all damage break Armour, and with Imploding Impacts lower enemy Armour below 0. Finally, Jade Heritage grants additional physical damage reduction and a powerful guard skill.
Gear priorities
Push the shield's Armour. Chest with Life + Armour + "Armour also applies to elemental damage". Attack speed on gloves, desecrated skill speed on jewellery. Thrillsteel as the unique.
Replacement candidates
Keep updating the shield and chest Armour while maintaining Life and resistances. Push gems to Lv20, 20% quality and 6 links.
Caution
If single-target damage is lacking, the source FAQ's solution is to swap Ahn's Citadel and Kaom's Madness for Concentrated Area and Close Combat II, and use Sunder on enemies whose Armour is fully broken. Modest scaling in the very endgame is a weakness in the source.
Completion condition
The finished form. From here, refine the shield and chest Armour and max-roll each gear piece.

BUILD PROFILE

Shield Wall Warbringer: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • Gear is easy to acquire and the build works with no uniques (source strength: Easy to acquire gear; the guide text states it functions without uniques).
  • Good damage and speed even on a low budget (source strength: Good damage and speed on low budget).
  • With Warcaller's Bellow, warcries also explode corpses, chaining your clear (source strength: Corpse explosions).
  • Extremely tanky against elemental hits (source summary: EXTREMELY tanky character impervious to elemental hits).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Animations are slow early on (source weakness: Slow animations early on).
  • Scaling in the very endgame is modest (source weakness: Lower scaling in the very endgame).
  • Single-target damage tends to fall short as-is, and for bosses you need to swap the lineage supports to Concentrated Area + Close Combat II (source FAQ).
  • The main skill, Shield Wall, cannot be used until Lv22, so you level with a two-handed mace early on.

Gear priorities

  1. Shield: the highest-Armour one (Shield Wall's damage scales with the shield's Armour. The source FAQ states explicitly that weapon damage is irrelevant to its damage).
  2. Chest: high Life and Armour. The "Armour also applies to elemental damage" mod has a large impact on durability.
  3. Everywhere: secure resistances up to 75% and stack Life. Attack speed on gloves and desecrated skill speed on jewellery improve feel and clear speed. Thrillsteel is a candidate unique.
