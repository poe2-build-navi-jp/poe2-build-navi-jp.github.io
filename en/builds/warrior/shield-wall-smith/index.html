PRACTICAL GUIDE

When you're unsure about Shield Wall's usage requirements, detonation, or boss fights

Cross-checked Lundburgerr's leveling guide text (title 0.5, tags 0.5.5 FR) against poe2db's usage requirements. The full support names for the later stages and actual in-game behavior have not been re-verified.

What if Shield Wall still can't be used at Lv22?

The Tier 7, gem Lv7 version requires character Lv22, 41 Strength and a shield with Armour. Higher-tier gems have different requirements. Check the equipped shield and the in-game gem display, and if a requirement is unmet, keep the previous stage's setup.

Does Shield Wall attack just by placing it?

The source's clear procedure is to place the wall and use a warcry or Shield Charge. The game data also explains destruction by your own corresponding skills. Separate placing the wall from detonating it, and check which skills your current stage uses.

When should I use Sunder in boss fights?

The source guides you to use Sunder after Armour Break. If you have not taken it, do not copy the finished form as-is; check your current stage's setup. Fortifying Cry is also a support added after you obtain it.

BUILD PROFILE

Shield Wall Smith: strengths, weaknesses and gear priorities

Organized from the published sources. Use it as a checklist before choosing and when replacing gear, not as a score.

Strengths

  • After obtaining the first ascendancy, durability is easy to get in the campaign.
  • Per-stage Act, Maps and Endgame setups are published.
  • Smith of Kitava's resistance support assumes the nodes taken and gear resistances. The ascendancy's general potential does not guarantee this build's durability in its finished form.
  • The build works with little gear investment, and from early maps it is easy to divert gear slots to item rarity (Kami-labs).

Weaknesses & cautions

  • Shield Wall's Tier 7, gem Lv7 version requires character Lv22, 41 Strength and a shield with Armour. In the source, you switch in Act 2 at Lv22 once the gem and shield conditions are met.
  • At high gear budgets, damage and clear do not scale as much as other setups, per the source.
  • In 0.5, Fortifying Cry's behavior changed, and single-target damage from Act 3 onward drops by 30% in the worst case (Kami-labs).
  • The Act 1 Rolling Slam → Boneshatter sequence requires practicing animation cancelling. Perfect Strike at Lv14 deals no damage if you miss the timing (Kami-labs).

Gear priorities

  1. Shield (most important): Armour value and increased Armour %. Shield Wall adds 5–7 physical damage per 15 shield Armour, so damage is also decided by the shield (poe2db; changed from 6–8 in 0.5.0). An Armour-based shield is required to use it.
  2. At the Lv22 switch: prepare a high-Armour shield in advance.
  3. Resistances: if your setup takes Coal Stoker, 50% of your Fire Resistance modifiers also apply to Cold and Lightning Resistance (current effect text on poe2db). Check the actual nodes taken in the source's per-stage trees, and do not build gear around this effect if the node is not taken.
  4. Once the required resistances are secured, spare suffixes can go to damage or item rarity (Maxroll / Kami-labs).
  5. Early maps: there is an option to use the unique Thrillsteel for permanent Onslaught to speed up mapping (Kami-labs).
  6. Armour pieces: Life and missing resistances. Use armour pieces that match your current level.
